# 2018 B4 references and published status

## Official starting sources

- [Paper B](https://www.simonmarais.org/uploads/8/2/3/5/82358688/smmc-2018-paper-b_1.pdf), B4 on page 2.
- [Official solutions](https://www.simonmarais.org/uploads/8/2/3/5/82358688/smmc-2018-solutions_1.pdf).

Official solutions (pp. 16-17) establish \(|A(5,n)|=(5n^2+5n+2)/2\). They do not supply a general resolution. Check all boundary cases, especially \(m=1,2\), and distinguish a tautological coefficient expression from a useful determination.
