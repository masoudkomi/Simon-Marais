# Coordinator acceptance of narrow Strong FC source objections

2026-10-16 UTC. **Specific source objections independently reviewed.**
This is not an acceptance or refutation of the intended Diophantine
theorem. No normalized nontrivial solution contradicting that theorem
has been produced. The preprint is not adopted as a proved exclusion
theorem in this project.

The coordinator read both complete initially blind whole-source reports,
their issue logs and post-freeze comparisons, and checked the actual
Definition4/Lemmas4–6 on PDF pp7–9. Both reviewers independently
reconstructed the whole10-page argument before reading the earlier
literature concerns. Their original reports remain unchanged. Source:
Preda Mihăilescu, [arXiv:2509.18275v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18275v1),
PDF SHA256 `daba36d8971a346d4cd40d0b823f787034c4a11b67e76258beeb7574e6fe2669`.
The following shared, precisely scoped findings are accepted.

## SO1: the printed ideal intersection does not impose all congruences

Definition4 takes an intersection of the ideals D0(sigma). In a Dedekind
domain its prime valuations are maxima; the next proof instead needs
minima, as in the ideal sum. A prime dividing the intersection need not
divide every term. Including the identity as printed makes that
intersection simply(w′). Omitting the identity alone does not repair
the inference. Replacing the intersection by a sum repairs this definition,
but does not supply Lemmas5–6. Prime-level congruences also cannot alone
establish all prime-power multiplicities or rational generation at ramified
primes. The reviewers identify local repairs at2 without treating them as
a repair of the full argument.

## SO2: separate divisibilities do not give the required product

In Lemma5, write b=beta(t)=b1*b2 and b−Z^k=D*chi, with(D,Z)=1.
The proof obtains b1|chi and b2|chi, then uses b1*b2|chi without proving
coprimality or the missing valuations. This is the missing integrality
needed before its otherwise valid Kronecker step.

The diagnosis survives granting the paper its intended repaired
characteristic-ideal setup. Let the conjugate nonunit ideals sigma(A) be
pairwise coprime with product(Z), and(beta(t))=A^t. For positive
t=sum n_sigma*sigma of relative weight2k, coefficients satisfy
n_sigma+n_(j*sigma)=2k. If t is not kN, some n_sigma>k. At a prime P
of that sigma(A), of multiplicity a>0,

    v_P(b)=n_sigma*a,  v_P(Z^k)=k*a,
    v_P(chi)=k*a<n_sigma*a,

because D is a unit there. Hence the asserted quotient chi/b is not
integral under those same hypotheses. A new argument is required.
Adding coprimality of positive equal-weight pieces forces complementary
supports and t=kN, losing the next nonrationality claim. This is a
conditional valuation diagnosis of a proof step, not an actual solution
or counterexample to the Diophantine equation.

## SO3: the permitted norm exponent defeats the stated nonrationality

The allowed J_k contains kN, already used elsewhere by the source. It gives
beta(kN)=Z^k and a rational norm power. Lemma1's pairwise coprimality
does not make that power nonrational. At p=5 both auditors independently
prove the stronger exact boundary J1={N}; merely invoking the source's
weight-two construction cannot supply a non-norm choice there.
This does not preclude a different nontriviality argument or another proof.

## SO4: relative splitting does not imply rational splitting

Lemma6 uses E intersect H′={1} to infer E contained in G′ in the direct
product Gal(Q(zeta_p,zeta_q)/Q)=G′×H′. These conditions differ. For
(p,q,r)=(5,3,2), Frobenius generates the subgroup generated by(2,2), of
order4. Its intersection with H′ is trivial but its projection to H′
is nontrivial. A prime of Q(zeta_5) above2 splits in the compositum over
that field, while2 is inert in Q(zeta_3). All these primes are unramified.
Thus the inference on those field-theoretic premises is false.

The valid weaker numerical conclusion is ord_q(r)|ord_p(r), not r=1
modq. Specialized information from the equation could in principle force
the missing projection condition, but the displayed subgroup argument
does not establish it. The example is not a global Strong FC solution.

## Boundaries of this acceptance and next reasoning

The intended nontrivial odd-prime domain is kept separate from literal
wording that also includes trivial solutions or fails to repeat q odd.
Those wording examples do not refute the intended claim. A valid theorem
would address an important signed Nagell–Ljunggren sector; the potential
strength of that consequence is itself neither evidence for nor against
the proof.

The two audits also record normalization, sign, module and denominator
issues, distinguishing repairs that are available from those still
missing. Their additional p11 local calculations are retained at their
individual audit scope; this acceptance does not silently promote every
new auxiliary claim in either report. Both grant precise standard
Jacobi-generator machinery when diagnosing the central obstructions;
neither claims to have retrieved and re-audited every original book or
1922/1975 source.

The useful next mathematical task is a replacement integral or splitting
argument with actual specialized hypotheses, not more test cases or a
declaration that the full conjecture has been disproved. No author or
organizer has been contacted. Exact initial/final hashes and the accepted
scope are preserved in the companion JSON and integrity record.
