## Exact L-value normalization and analytic authority

For a nontrivial odd character psi modulo the prime p, its conductor
is exactly p, and it is primitive. Extend it by zero at p-multiples.

    sum_a psi(a) zeta_p^(an)=bar(psi)(n) tau(psi)

for p not dividing n; for p|n both sides are zero. Expanding the
squared modulus of tau with a=tb gives an inner sum p-1 at t=1
and -1 otherwise. Since sum_t psi(t)=0, |tau(psi)|^2=p.

For 0<rho<1 the logarithm series is absolutely convergent and may
be summed over the finite a-set. This yields

    sum_(n>=1) bar(psi)(n) rho^n/n
       =-tau(psi)^-1 sum_a psi(a) log(1-rho*zeta_p^a).

The coefficient sequence is periodic of mean zero. Its bounded
partial sums and the decreasing weights rho^n/n bound the tail
uniformly by a constant/N for 0<rho<=1. Thus the Abel limit is
the convergent L(1,bar(psi)). Every logarithm segment stays in the
right half-plane, so the continued branch is the principal one.

the real logarithms, and sum psi=0 cancels the constant imaginary
part. With B_(1,psi)=p^-1 sum_a a psi(a), this gives

    L(1,bar(psi))=-i*pi*B_(1,psi)/tau(psi),
    |B_(1,psi)|=sqrt(p)/pi*|L(1,bar(psi))|.           (C)
