## Current-literature audit

Checked 2026-10-16. No independently verified unrestricted resolution was located; a search is not proof of absence. The contemporary restricted publications below and an unreviewed full-solution claim were examined. See computations/literature-audit.md for objections. Published imports have not been fully re-proved or independently audited by this project.

1. Michael A. Bennett and Aaron Levin, *The Nagell-Ljunggren equation via Runge's method*, Monatshefte fuer Mathematik 177 (2015), 15-31, DOI [10.1007/s00605-015-0748-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00605-015-0748-1). [Author-posted published PDF](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~bennett/BeLe-Mona-2015.pdf); [arXiv 1312.4037](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.4037). Domain |x|,|y|>1, m>2, q>=2; Proposition 1 and published Theorem 1, pp. 16-17. The arXiv version numbers the main theorem 2. See research.md for the translated restrictions and sign normalization.
2. Yann Bugeaud and Preda Mihailescu, *On the Nagell-Ljunggren equation*, Mathematica Scandinavica 101 (2007), 177-183, DOI [10.7146/math.scand.a-15038](https://doi.org/10.7146/math.scand.a-15038). [Journal page](https://journals.msp.org/mscand/article/view/659), [primary PDF](https://journals.msp.org/mscand/article/download/659/658). Theorem NL, Theorems 1-2, pp. 177-179. Domain x,y>1, m>2, q>=2. The 29 bound is therefore not an unrestricted negative-base claim.
3. Tran Duc Son, *On the Nagell-Ljunggren equation (x^5-1)/(x-1)=y^3*, Integers 26 (2026), A44, published 2026-04-03, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.19403779](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19403779). [Original journal PDF](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/aa44/aa44.pdf). Theorem 3 concerns degree 4 and q=3 only. Its all-integer conclusion is false as printed: (-1,1) is an exact counterexample. No reliance is placed on it.
4. Han Chen and Preda Mihailescu, *New Conditions on the Nagell-Ljunggren Equation*, Chinese Annals of Mathematics, Series B 47 (2026), 389-412, published 2026-03-07, DOI [10.1007/s11401-026-0018-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11401-026-0018-7). [Publisher abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11401-026-0018-7); [journal abstract](https://journal.hep.com.cn/caoms/EN/10.1007/s11401-026-0018-7). Abstract reports nonexistence for (x^p-1)/(x-1)=p^e y^q with distinct odd primes p,q, p>3, and q not dividing the minus cyclotomic class number h_p^-. Full theorem domains and ancillary hypotheses have not been checked; this is a lead, not an applied theorem.
5. Cheng-Ting Wang, *On Some Properties of ABC Triples and Radicals of an Integer*, unreviewed preprint v1, posted 2025-12-02, DOI [10.20944/preprints202512.0286.v1](https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202512.0286.v1). [Original full text](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202512.0286/v1). Claims a positive Nagell-Ljunggren classification via Theorem 7's abc bound; a false equality in its supporting equation (33) prevents accepting that proof. Negative x is outside its claimed result in any event.
