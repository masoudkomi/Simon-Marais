# DR8 genuine failed routes and remaining arithmetic gap

Candidate proof - unverified.18 October2026UTC. These were hand
research routes, not executable scans. No mathematical program ran.

1. **Pure p-th-power base gap.** A first possible exclusion when X
   itself is a signed p-th power is exactly within accepted PG1.
   I read that full accepted body and discarded this as new progress.
   It is not a new DR8 theorem or a proof premise.
2. **Stop at a neighbouring perfect-power gap.** Shifted real-power
   intervals can treat a convenient adjacent-power family, but do not
   constrain a general actual large c. This was not taken as the
   requested substantive bridge. The complete actual factorization
   of Phi_p(X)-1 gives a stronger route without a power-shaped c.
3. **Optimize another height estimate for J.** DR7's J and its sign
   may sharpen bounds in selected real ranges, but still leave b
   unbounded. I did not present such a change of constants as a new
   arithmetic solution or upper cofactor theorem.
4. **More Taylor congruences alone.** Higher ordinary divisibility
   can give additional necessary relations between J,b,Q and m.
   They do not force a sign or height contradiction when c grows.
   No unverified higher-congruence assertion is imported here.
5. **All primes of the other distance divide Y-1.** This is not a
   justified inference from Y^p1 modulo that distance. The exact
   order1/orderp split matters. If its orderp part were1, THEN the
   entire distance would divide Y-1, contradicting ordinary size.
   Keeping that actual part gives e>Q, rather than a false premise.
6. **Generic Zsigmondy prime.** A primitive prime for Phi_d(X) need
   not have orderp for Y and could lie in Y-1. The new proof first
   forces a factor OUTSIDE Y-1 by size, then uses its actual orderp
   to eliminate anomalous small index-prime behaviour. No Zsigmondy
   or BHV theorem is invoked without checking its hypotheses.
7. **All cyclotomic values are pairwise coprime.** False in general.
   Small primes dividing related indices can repeat. Only the new
   filtered R_d are pairwise coprime, since their primes are>p>d
   and the exact orders of X distinguish the indices.
8. **Each component gets an independent full budget.** The actual
   order1 factors satisfy productT_d=T=(Y1)/(Qd0), not a collection
   of independently assignable integers up to Y1. The joint identity
   is retained. It is not proved that its large-support complement
   cannot occur at an actual integer point.
9. **Count Phi_p(Y)'s primes as Y's primes.** Invalid. The new count
   is for the next cyclotomic value, and no known upper bound matching
   it is supplied. It does not imply a bounded p or finite annual list.
10. **Transfer the whole result to X not0,-1 modp.** Then p can
    divide H and a component, so the present p-free partition needs
    a changed argument. No such extension is claimed.
11. **Discard DR7's prime range.** b>1 is a separately accepted
    p>=37 statement. It is not assumed for the p3 known cases and
    was not checked by a larger table. The new factor transfer is
    stated within precisely the accepted ordinary residual sector.
12. **Class-unit or few-digit theorem supplies the missing upper
    bound.** Primary sources have different sign, fixed-base, common-
    divisor, exponent or class-number hypotheses. None was silently
    applied to the actual large components here. Root's unverified
    formal higher-trace warning is not an actual Jacobi element or
    a premise, and norm1 alone gives no higher-order contact.

The usable new bridge is an exact decomposition of Phi_p(Y)/p into
two actual adjacent factors and a nontrivial, disjoint factor for
EVERY d|p-1,d>2, with simultaneous actual orders and a single order1
budget. The remaining inference is equally precise: find an applicable
upper support/size restriction or an incompatibility for these SAME
actual integers. No such upper bound or contradiction is proved.
Large formal factors meeting the displayed conditions are not actual
solutions; nor does their formal possibility refute a Diophantine
conjecture. No annual or priority claim follows from this checkpoint.
