    V+V^(-1)−2=(V−1)^2/V.

Thus normalized real trace has local contact2t while its integral

## A hand formal obstruction to the norm-only fourfold inference

Work formally in C(T)/C(T^4), with sigma(T)=iT, j=sigma² and valuation
ord_T. Put L(T)=T^3+T^5 and the RATIONAL function

    V(T)=(1+L(T)/2)/(1−L(T)/2).

Then V(−T)=1/V(T), ord_T(V−1)=3, and the order-four subgroup norm is1
because both j-pairs have product1. Write V_a=V(aT). Exact algebra gives

    V(T)+V(−T)−2=L(T)^2/[1−L(T)^2/4].

Now

    L(T)^2=T^6+2T^8+T^10,
    L(iT)^2=−T^6+2T^8−T^10.

Each denominator correction begins at order6, so the sum of the two
opposite-pair traces satisfies

    sum_(a in{1,−1,i,−i}) V(aT)−4=4T^8+O(T^12).

not a numerical experiment. It only challenges the generic assertion
