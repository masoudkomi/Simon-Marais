# R9 failed routes and exact remaining inference

2026-10-18 UTC. Candidate proof - unverified. This records real attempted
inferences, not an assertion that all possible approaches fail.

1. **Many orbit columns plus a small homogeneous vector.** R9A obtains
   genuinely polynomial coefficients for the ACTUAL phase system. It
   does not ensure the evaluated delta is nonzero. The pigeonhole vector
   may be dense; carrying over the sparse cofactor Wronskian order would
   be a false improvement. The complete main uses n_*=2q m instead.

2. **An ordinary Siegel estimate plus codimension one.** A small first
   minimum can lie entirely in the forbidden subspace. R9C gives exact
   rational counterexamples even when every indicated perturbed kernel
   has a unit vector. A valid avoidance theorem has its own height cost;
   the full cofactor substitute(17) is not a polynomial-height repair.
   These generic examples are not asserted to be actual Jacobi matrices.

3. **Two separately dividing Jacobi factors.** Their common ideal support
   cannot be ignored. For every non-N binary half pair the overlap prime
   has valuations e,e,e in b1,b2,Y. The quotient intended to be an integral
   unit has a pole. R9D is conditional on actual ideal hypotheses; it is
   not an actual solution refuting a source theorem. R9F demonstrates the
   failure at original weight with genuine cyclotomic numbers while
   retaining, and explicitly failing, the missing linear p-th-power input.

4. **Use the finite-2 Frobenius kernel at infinity.** R9E works for actual
   arithmetic data but has relative weight6. The unique norm shift to
   original weight has a coefficient-2. No positivity-preserving lifting
   to R8's columns, complex phase purity, or all-conjugate infinity
   cancellation follows. A mod2 equality and an analytic branch at1/X
   are distinct constraints.

5. **Read an obsolete preprint as the current source.** The 2021 v1 has
   a carry/small-vector route; the current 2025 v4 has another author and
   a different ideal/splitting proof. Current v4 was then read ENTIRELY.
   The small-vector objection is not attributed to v4. Conversely the
   current product inference is not called a defect in every earlier
   paper. No theorem is accepted merely from its statement or abstract.

6. **Distinct ideals imply additive independence.** Pairwise-distinct
   Jacobi ideals do not prove an additive normal basis or prevent a
   many-term evaluated relation over Kplus. All selected values already
   belong to K, while their formal radical functions have much larger
   monodromy. Formal independence does not survive specialization by
   assertion. A prime valuation can exclude a relation with a unique
   minimum term, but no such minimum is forced for the actual many-term
   kernel. No normal-basis theorem with verified applicable hypotheses
   was found or imported.

7. **Positive two-term norm expressions.** Half-weight roots permit
   |b_a-zeta^k b_b|^2, nonzero when the two ideals differ. A global k can
   align phases at selected embeddings; the existing free-orbit count
   does not force enough aligned embeddings for the desired norm bound.
   The source's actual signature must be retained.

8. **Generic resultant or Runge shortcut.** An algebraic-function norm
   can have exponentially large Kummer degree; no useful zero-branch
   improvement was proved by it. On y^p=Phi_p(x), the coprime degrees
   p,p-1 give one place at infinity in the elementary model. No applicable
   multiple-infinity-place Runge theorem was supplied. This is a limit
   of the attempted argument, not a proof no refined method exists.

9. **Another short-vector counting argument will force escape.** Counting
   binary images supplies many small relations but does not show that
   they span the full kernel, or that evaluation is nonzero on that
   collection. The precise unresolved statement is an actual, controlled
   escape from the evaluated equality subspace, OR a strong enough bound
   on a genuine first nonzero zero-branch coefficient. It must use the
   linear characteristic p-th power or other genuine arithmetic input.

The new source obstruction and polynomial conditional branch are useful
partial mathematics, not a substitute for this remaining bridge. The
known published diagonal bounds and class-number conditions are stronger
than the huge unconditional zero-branch bound retained here.
