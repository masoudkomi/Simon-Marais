## 1. Distinct ideals imply an arbitrary linear relation is nonzero

That remains false as a general inference. The new proof uses equality
of TWO actual conjugates to force equality of their full exponent
vectors, then M1 to force identical indices. It makes beta a primitive
element and a PRODUCT of pairwise differences nonzero. It does NOT
prove a normal basis, full phase rank, or that an old many-term R9
linear delta escapes its evaluated equality subspace. This is a
different deliberately chosen auxiliary, not a repair by renaming.

## 2. V has unit-circle conjugates, so Disc(V) is an integer

V=beta/Y may have finite poles. Its discriminant is rational but need
not be integral. The actual integer begins with Disc(beta), and its
only proposed division is by an explicitly guaranteed power of the
ordinary integer Y. Every rational prime of that denominator is
checked using the actual characteristic ideal and all pair minima.
No global-integral-unit or Kronecker conclusion is applied to V.

## 3. Divide out Y using separate divisibilities

The old R9 overlap obstruction forbids that shortcut. The new exponent
M is not guessed from two factors separately dividing a difference.
At EACH prime over ell|Y the complete valuation multiset is known;
sum ALL min-valuations across ALL squared differences. That gives
the guaranteed exponent eM in the rational discriminant, even when
some equal-valuation differences cancel further. No equality or
unproved integrality of a quotient ideal generator is needed.

## 4. Norm(A)=Y means exactly one prime over ell automatically

Not for an arbitrary ideal. Here the extra ACTUAL pairwise conjugate
coprimality proves it: two primes in A would be related by a nonidentity
Galois element and give a shared prime in two conjugates. Complete
splitting then makes its valuation exactly v_ellY. Without that step
the stated multiset and division exponent would be unjustified.

BR has v_p Norm(alpha)=v_lambda(alpha) because the residue degree

## 6. The first leading-residue collision gives an exact next order

## 7. The field discriminant alone is enough

Its ordinary cyclotomic lower power would be far weaker. The useful

the polynomial discriminant highly divisible. Primitive degree is
also essential; a repeated-root product would have been zero, not
an integer of positive absolute value.

kept ONLY when an actual raw-phase collision factor epsilon^C can
