## Signed off-diagonal theorem: the exception survives full-text inspection

Chen–Mihăilescu, [*New Conditions on the Nagell-Ljunggren Equation*](https://journal.hep.com.cn/caoms/EN/10.1007/s11401-026-0018-7), 2026, full24-page paper: equation1.3 concerns (x^p−1)/(x−1)=p^e y^q, |x|,|y|>1, distinct odd primes p,q, p>=5, with e determined by x≡1 modp. Theorem1.2, printedp390 (PDFp2), excludes solutions outside its listed set S when q does not divide the relative minus class number h_p^−, **except possibly p<29 and x<0**. Footnote1 explains that the29 bound used at places came from a positive-x source. It cannot be dropped because an abstract says the criteria are sign-independent. Theorem1.1 gives the older e=1 class-number criterion. Theorem1.3 attributes to earlier work a separate exclusion for q>(p−1)²; its original2007 proof was not obtained in this pass, so this is a verified later restatement rather than a newly audited import.

## The diagonal class-number route and a later computational input

Mihăilescu, [*Class Number Conditions for the Diagonal Case of the Equation of Nagell and Ljunggren*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-211-74280-8_15), Dev. Math.16 (2008),245–273, studies integer x,y in (x^p−1)/(x−1)=p^e y^p. The publicly [author-posted full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/226863681_Class_Number_Conditions_for_the_Diagonal_Case_of_the_Equation_of_Nagell_and_Ljunggren), version2.0 dated2007-09-17, states: Theorem1's first case requires p-rank r>sqrt(p)−1 in the relative class group; Theorem2 imposes simultaneous base2 and3 Wieferich congruences; Theorem4, p>=17, makes the second case require failure of Vandiver. Its first/second case division uses x modp outside/inside {−1,0,1}. These are necessary conditions, not a general solution. The old numerical exclusion uses class-group computations through12million. No successful PDF cache of this chapter was obtained here; the author-uploaded text was read. A mathematical source edition should be checked before importing detailed bounds.

Bartolomé–Mihăilescu, [*On the equation X^n−1=BZ^n*, arXiv:1412.5798v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.5798v2), published IJNT (2017), is a useful nearby theorem with a crucial parameter hypothesis. Theorem1.2 assumes B>1, n prime, gcd(n,φ(radB))=1 and a nontrivial integer solution; it deduces n>163·10^6 and cyclotomic conditions. Its equation5 is diagonal Nagell–Ljunggren; Theorem2.2 bounds |X| for n>=17 in three residue cases. Remark1.4 explicitly says colleagues could not verify a singular-case computation in the2008 argument and supplies Appendix computations to address it. This is a source repair trail, not grounds for silently treating all versions as identical. The cached PDF is arXivv2 but its internal version date is2021-11-02; submission metadata and internal revision date are distinguished.

Hart–Harvey–Ong, [*Irregular primes to two billion*, arXiv:1605.02398v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.02398), Math. Comp.86 (2017),3031–3049, reports computations for **every odd prime p<2³¹**. They verify Vandiver and λ_p=ν_p=i_p, which identifies the p-part of the cyclotomic class group as an elementary abelian group of rank i_p at the first level. The maximum i_p found is9. This is a published finite computation, not unrestricted Vandiver, and rerunning its8.6million core-hours is out of scope.

**Candidate consequence to check and review:** combine the exact diagonal theorems with Hart–Harvey–Ong to replace the old12million/163million computational range by p<2³¹. For p>=101, i_p<=9 contradicts r>sqrt(p)−1 if the source's r is indeed identified with that p-part rank; Vandiver handles the second case. Lower primes need the existing small-exponent results. This would be a consequence of earlier work, not a novel broad classification. The source identification, nontrivial/signed boundaries and original repair appendix must be checked before asserting the corollary. The p-part computation does **not** exclude an off-diagonal q dividing h_p^− when q differs from p.

## A stronger 2025 preprint claim, kept separate from accepted results

Preda Mihăilescu, [*The strong Fermat-Catalan Equation*, arXiv:2509.18275v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18275v1), 2025-09-22, ten pages, Theorem1 claims nonexistence of nontrivial coprime integer solutions of (X^p+Y^p)/(X+Y)=p^e Z^q for p>3 and q distinct odd primes. No journal acceptance, correction, or withdrawal was verified. The entire PDF was read; it is not accepted as a theorem here.

If valid with its stated integer domain, the claim would cover signed prime-term off-diagonal Nagell–Ljunggren: positive x uses (X,Y)=(x,−1); negative x=−a uses (X,Y)=(a,1), because p is odd. In both cases a coordinate is±1, so coprimality is automatic. This is a potential prior-work match much stronger than the2026 class-number theorem. It does not cover p=q, composite term counts without reduction, or the contest's separately treated boundary values.

**Candidate source concern, unverified, not a refutation:** Lemma5, pp8–9, infers divisibility by β(t1)β(t2) from separate divisibility by β(t1) and β(t2). This inference generally needs coprimality or a valuation estimate. The displayed decomposition t=t1+t2 does not itself state the needed disjoint-support hypothesis. The later exclusion of α^(2tp) from Z also needs care because norm-group-ring elements yield rational powers; the paper itself uses kN in J_k. A specialized argument may select t to avoid these problems, so this observation is a request for exact justification, not a counterexample to the theorem. Two fresh independent audits would be required before promoting either an objection or the claimed result.
