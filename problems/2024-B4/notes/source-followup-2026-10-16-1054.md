# Primary-literature follow-up, 2026-10-16

The 2014 Férard full theorem remains unread. Further ordinary web searches
for an author-posted copy, including the precise title, DOI and HAL record,
did not locate a usable full paper. The GAATI members page lists the author
without a personal-page link. No author was contacted. This is a bounded
search outcome, not evidence of novelty or absence of public copies.

A related primary publisher record was inspected:

Éric Férard, *On the irreducibility of the hyperplane sections of Fermat
varieties in P^3 in characteristic 2. II*, Cryptography and Communications
9 (2017), 749–767,
[DOI 10.1007/s12095-017-0213-1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12095-017-0213-1).
Its abstract describes sufficient conditions for absolute irreducibility
when t=1 mod4, involving the odd part of t-1. The precise bound and excluded
form are not given in that abstract. The publisher identifies the full
text as subscription content and offers paid access. No purchase,
subscription, login or access bypass was attempted. The full theorem's
hypotheses therefore remain unverified and are not used in any proof.

Useful original-site links: [GAATI publications](https://gaati.org/publications/fr/)
and [GAATI members](https://gaati.org/members/en/). The public-record follow-up
does not resolve the exact prior attribution of the fixed-factor criterion.
