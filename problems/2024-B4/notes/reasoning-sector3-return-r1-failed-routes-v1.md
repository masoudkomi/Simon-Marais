# Failed or narrowed routes retained

2026-10-17 UTC. All proposed mathematical claims remain candidate proof -
unverified. These failures are not counterexamples to the published sector
theorem or to a theorem that was never asserted.

1. **General HM component theorem -> whole irreducibility.** Invalid.
   The global theorem supplies an F2-defined absolutely irreducible
   component. The polynomial can have other components; the paper itself
   distinguishes its false whole conjecture from the weaker result.
   The original-author 3mod4 proof is a DIFFERENT, stronger sector result.

2. **HM Theorem15 proves missing F2 irreducibility.** Circular. Its
   stated ordinary-irreducibility premise must already be established.
   Its divisibility case also does not cover i1,ell>=3. Neither it nor
   Theorem16 is a replacement for the exact older theorem.

3. **Use an abstract as the proof source.** The publisher abstract
   suggested the correct whole statement, but did not establish the
   operative local hypotheses. Normal journal/thesis web routes failed.
   Existing ignored cached thesis pages were then located and the actual
   theorem, proof, PropositionA and definitions were visually read. No
   successful new download or journal-PDF read is claimed.

4. **A double tangent by itself excludes component intersection.**
   False: two local factors can have the same tangent. The cubic must
   miss that tangent. Equations(2)--(3) supply that missing condition.
   A source statement that the cubic has distinct factors would by
   itself also be insufficient: its factors could include the tangent.

5. **An affine classification automatically handles infinity.** Not
   valid without an argument. The candidate uses symmetry to place
   EACH projective point into an actual affine chart. This replaces,
   rather than silently assumes, the source's terse infinity remark.
   Root also withdrew an imprecise binom(n,2) rationale for R-corners;
   the present proof needs no such rationale. A later proposed t13
   infinity counterexample (1:a:0), a a primitive cube root, was ALSO
   withdrawn: checking only X,Y partials missed the homogeneous Z
   partial, which is (1+a)^12=1. In fact the source infinity assertion
   is correct, by partial_Z f_n=(X+Y)^(n-1) away from R and the three
   boundary vertices with phi_n=1. No source defect is claimed.

6. **Coprime Bézout alone excludes repeated factors.** Insufficient.
   The proof separately includes the common-component case, where two
   positive homogeneous factors meet along that component. The same
   local contradiction applies. No prior squarefree conclusion is
   required in the sector reconstruction.

7. **Coordinate-boundary smoothness transports to a diagonal of R.**
   This would be wrong. For arbitrary target n the diagonal can have
   multiplicity Q-1 or Q. S3B computes its actual numerator and quotient
   terms, then treats the two different tangent patterns separately.

8. **Boundary divisibility alone makes the cofactor a unit.** Not
   automatic. An initial weaker idea used an even contact at a primitive
   cube root on Y0; this would only immediately handle certain bases.
   The final argument chooses any nontrivial ell-th root on Y=Z,
   explicitly excludes the a in F_Q tangent case, and proves unitness
   in the other case by consecutive homogeneous terms. It therefore
   handles EVERY base m>3,3mod4, not only m7 or 7mod12.

9. **Discard the denominator correction.** It could change a proof
   based on a quotient coefficient. Formula(8) retains it exactly as
   (b/c³)y^Q. The x^Q coefficient survives for a structural reason:
   both the correction and the relevant initial quotient contain y.

10. **Composition indecomposability is needed for phi7 saturation.**
    It is unnecessary here. Once the PUBLISHED whole phi7 absolute
    irreducibility is reconstructed, a common absolute component is
    already the entire phi7. The same applies uniformly to all bases
    in this sector. CS's distinct Kasami saturation proof stays intact.

11. **Isolated sector factors classify all reducible exponents.** No.
    The remaining 1mod4 polynomials may have components of other kinds.
    A necessary exponent congruence and an exact fixed-family gcd are
    scoped partial results. At equality n=m the cofactor is a unit;
    it does not create an official two-factor example.

12. **Finite coefficient or exponent scans establish the universal
