# FR10 failed or deliberately unclaimed routes

**Candidate proof - unverified.** These are real inference limits, not a
substitute for the positive actual-factor proof in the main.

1. **One tangent does not imply one component.** F_q=L^q cannot alone
   exclude an intersection. If the next form is squarefree and includes
   L, an actual smooth branch and a branch of orderq-1 can meet. The new
   local calculation counts EXACT intersectionq, retaining this case.
2. **R9-J cannot be assumed from a small gcd.** No theorem asserting this
   was established or used. In the final ell35 family J actually fails
   by the F8 affine coset, while the linear intersection budget suffices.
3. **Not all off-diagonal points are ordinary.** Squaring/Frobenius
   coefficient identities must distinguish F_q=0 from F_q!=0. The exact
   normalized ratio argument in main3 replaces any informal table use.
4. **A multiplicity-product lower bound is not an upper bound.** At
   nonordinary points tangent cones share L, so the usual I>=uv cannot
   support the global upper estimate. The derivative-on-smooth-branch
   calculation independently proves the required upper/equalityq.
5. **Quotient jets do not inherit numerator gaps coefficientwise.** On
   diagonals the denominator has order1; its w2 correction is explicitly
   retained. Only the pure u^q coefficient survives the required check.
   At off-diagonal points R is a unit and is absorbed into a factor.
6. **Equal degrees/Frobenius descent were not assumed.** Ordinary F2
   irreducibility would impose an orbit structure on absolute components,
   but it is not the available hypothesis when seeking F2 reducibility.
   Choosing a least actual component avoids that false premise. Its full
   complement, with any internal factors, is retained in Bezout.
7. **Root-subgroup examples did not establish overlap automatically.**
   Informal probes of ell=q-3, q-2^s-1 and norm-one subgroups did not prove
   whether the old J/NF4 criteria cover every such member. No conclusion
   from those probes appears in the proof. The ell35 family instead has
   an exact four-point witness that both hypotheses fail.
8. **No global source defect is alleged.** A ratio-defined type in the
   classical source tables is unnecessary here: with a+b+c=1, vanishing
   leading coefficients directly force a,b,c in F_q. This direct proof
   does not depend on declaring a published table false or promoting a
   broad source objection from an apparently redundant subcase.
9. **Published component theorem is insufficient.** Having at least one
   F2-defined absolute component is consistent with ordinary reducibility.
   The paper's exceptional-APN conclusion is not a shortcut to FR10B.
10. **Source access did not yield a priority conclusion.** Jedlicka's
    operative original PDF returned403 and the mirror timed out. Férard
    operative articles remain unread. The hand proof is submitted with
    overlap unresolved, not with a claim that the criterion is new.
11. **The numerical condition has no converse.** P<=0 leaves the argument
    silent. q2,ell1,n1/n3 and the ell3q4 exception are explicit boundaries;
    no larger family or full ordinary classification is inferred.
12. **No computational evidence is a hidden premise.** No root scan,
    finite factor table, program, CAS, source executable, author check or
    old certificate was run. All signs, counts and finite-field witnesses
    in the main are hand proofs; SHA/JSON code is integrity only.
