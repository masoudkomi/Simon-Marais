# Férard source lead and a conditional residue-class reduction

2026-10-16 UTC. **Unverified literature lead, not an accepted classification.**
The [publisher's public abstract](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/amc.2014.8.497)
for Éric Férard's 2014 paper links non-absolute irreducibility in the
t=5 modulo8 regime to divisibility by phi13. A renewed search of author
and institutional sources found the same HAL record, but no verified
public full text. The earlier publisher login response is respected;
no purchase, login bypass, or alternative restricted download was tried.
The exact theorem and exceptions remain unread.

The distinction matters even before checking any deep argument. A literal
unqualified reading at t=5 cannot be correct: phi5=Q has degree2 and
factors over F4 as

    Q=(x+w*y+w^2*z)(x+w^2*y+w*z),  w^2+w+1=0.

Thus phi5 is not absolutely irreducible, while the degree10 phi13 cannot
divide it. This is a warning about missing abstract qualifications, not
a refutation of the paper's actual unread theorem. No conclusion about
the needed t>13 hypotheses is inferred from the abstract alone.

Here is the useful conditional target to verify in full text. **If** the
paper proves that, for every t>13 with t=5 modulo8, non-absolute
irreducibility implies whole-phi13 divisibility, the project's already
reviewed exact divisor criterion would give

    phi_t reducible over F2 with two factors of degree>=2
      iff t=(64*16^m+1)/5, m>=1,

in that residue class and range. Forward: such reducibility prevents
absolute irreducibility, so the hypothetical source theorem applies,
and the accepted divisor criterion gives the index form. Reverse: the
reviewed whole-phi13 factor has degree10 and, for m>=1, its complementary
factor has degree t-13>=2. No absolute/F2 field distinction is reversed.
This conditional implication does not verify its unexamined source premise.
The small t=5 and13 cases require separate treatment; neither is silently
included in the asserted range.

The [2017 sequel's public abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12095-017-0213-1)
offers sufficient absolute-irreducibility conditions when the odd part
of t-1 is sufficiently large and avoids a specified form. Its exact
conditions are absent from the abstract. The publisher explicitly labels
the full article subscription content; no paid access was attempted.
This remains a source lead, not an input to an accepted theorem.

The existing exact phi13 proof is self-contained and accepted regardless
of these literature leads. Attribution and the scope of a possible prior
classification should be revised only after the exact full statement and
its hypotheses are checked.
