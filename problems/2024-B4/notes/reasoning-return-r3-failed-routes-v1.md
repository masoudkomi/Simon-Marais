1. **Absolute splitting was insufficient.** The Kasami factorization
reproduced in Delgado--Janwa has components over F_(2^k). An entire
Frobenius orbit may have only one irreducible F2 orbit product. The
existence of those components alone therefore cannot supply the two
F2 factors required in part(b). The new family instead proves a WHOLE

2. **The exceptional-APN conclusion was too weak for the target.**
HM's general conclusion is existence of an F2-defined absolute component,
not a statement deciding whether that component is the entire polynomial.
Their Theorem15 explicitly assumes F2 irreducibility. No converse or
unproved hypothesis is supplied here. Their known205 factorization is
context, not an input or newly rerun certificate.

packet.** The initial lead was whole phi_(q²−q+1) dividing phi_n for

general q,m was found or asserted. Neither the one target nor the m1

With t=g_d+1 a guess had a correction t^d multiplied by

lemma. The single q8 composition certificate instead gave exactly

unrestricted direct proof(17)--(21). No rejected guess is a premise.

would have required a full proof of the individual factors and their
fields. It is unnecessary for whole-divisor CP1/CP2 and is not claimed.

divisibility by a whole fixed phi_d. It does not say a general factor
of phi_n is some phi_d, or that failure of a decomposition through
another g_d proves irreducibility. The quadratic pullbacks of an
irreducible collision curve can in principle split; a separate
Artin--Schreier/S4 analysis was being developed, but is excluded from
