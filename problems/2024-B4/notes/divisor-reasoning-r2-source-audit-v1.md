## Prior gcd results: what was actually checked

[Delgado--Janwa, arXiv:1207.5528v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.5528v1),
printed p. 5, Lemma 1, gives gcd statements for specified exponent
families, including distinct Welch exponents. Its arbitrary-odd-index
conclusion is not C7 or C11. I read the lemma, its stated decomposition
premises and short proof; no theorem about an entire APN classification
was substituted.

[Delgado--Janwa, arXiv:1602.02576v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.02576v1),
Theorem 10 and its surrounding scope, printed pp. 8--10, place one
designated exceptional family on one side of the gcd. This is a useful
published unrestricted gcd result, but 7 and 11 are not eligible as that
designated fixed divisor. Only the scope and theorem premises were used;
the preliminary construction is not reproduced here.

[Anbar--Kalayci--Yurdakul, WCC 2022](https://www.wcc2022.uni-rostock.de/storages/uni-rostock/Tagungen/WCC2022/Papers/WCC_2022_paper_64.pdf),
abstract, definitions and Lemma 1 on pp. 1--3, distinguishes the existence
of an absolute component from irreducibility of the whole homogeneous
curve. Its later theorem headings concern perturbed polynomial families.
It does not supply the exact two singleton sets in the passages checked.
I did not audit its later proofs, and no theorem from it is needed here.

The original Janwa--Wilson 1993 chapter remains unread in full. The
Kasami decomposition stated in
[Delgado--Janwa 2016, arXiv:1612.05997v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.05997v1)
does not directly answer either new fixed-divisor question. The earlier
packet's documented parity error in a later proof there is not a premise
of C7 or C11. No repaired or expanded source theorem is presumed.

## Recurrences and zero-set computability: attributed, not rediscovered

[Derksen, arXiv:math/0510583v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0510583v1),
Theorems 1.8--1.10, printed pp. 3--4, and Theorem 4.14 on printed p. 20
apply to recurrences over positive characteristic fields, with effective
determination for finitely generated fields. I checked these statements,
their definitions and the effective-comparison argument on printed p. 25.
The candidate's function field is explicitly finitely generated over
F2, and its four-root recurrence is elementary. This gives a general
fixed-factor reduction, not the explicit output set without calculation.
The full 43-page proof was not independently audited.

[Adamczewski--Bell, arXiv:1205.4091v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1205.4091v1),
Theorems 1.4--1.5, printed pp. 5--7, prove automaticity and effective
determination of zero coefficients of algebraic power series in positive
characteristic. This confirms that effectiveness of the zero-set method
is published background. No source claim that ordinary coefficient
values form a finite-valued automatic sequence over F2(b) is made.
The paper's full proof is not audited or needed for the explicit graph.
