# D7 real failed routes and the remaining arithmetic inference

18 October 2026 UTC. Candidate proof - unverified. These are exact
logical limits of the attempted ACTUAL inverse-mass route, not evidence
of an infinite avoiding ordinary start. No computational search was run.

1. **More parents automatically make many small counterexamples.**
   Incorrect. For a proper collateral parent along x, its full official
   ancestry belongs to A_n. These sets are disjoint, and their minimum
   Lambda escapes every fixed band. A second parent at a very late
   clock cannot be treated as a second chain reaching the original
   small clock. The depth deficit n-1-K_n tends to infinity if such
   clocks are infinite. A counting argument must pay that deficit.

2. **Avoidance for a long block forces a branch inside a fixed block.**
   False if it only assumes finite avoidance and is independent of the
   state size. The X_N family first hits at N+2 and has no second
   ordinary parent for ANY output during its whole avoiding prefix.
   Thus a uniform block argument of this form cannot be iterated to
   answer the ordinary problem. It remains possible that a theorem
   genuinely using an infinite avoiding future could force branches;
   this packet neither proves nor refutes that stronger theorem.

3. **Every full ancestor of an avoiding tail is a counterexample.**
   False as an inference. It may already have touched the diagonal.
   Restrict to Omega ancestry to transfer the never-hit property.
   A maximum-depth avoiding source can then have an actual diagonal
   parent. These births cannot be dropped when counting sources.

4. **All these are Q's strip orphans.** Wrong domain. The new graph is
   the FULL positive/product domain. A Q orphan has an ordinary parent
   outside the strip, possibly still official. Conversely a positive-
   domain source need not be inside the strip at its own clock. Q's
   exact accepted rank and inside-strip seed equivalence are not being
   replaced or enlarged here. Both source mechanisms remain unbounded.

5. **Same Lambda supplies a hitting comparison orbit.** Missing. CF
   gives eventual translation, not vanishing displacement. A rectangle
   of permanent translations need not preserve diagonal contact. The
   permanent even-a unit twin is an actual counterexample to merger,
   and CF6 gives no unconditional canonical or ordinary existence.

6. **Scaling the conditional ceiling orbit makes too many canonical
   words.** No. The permitted finite integer scalings/translation
   rectangles reproduce the already accepted bounded fibers in their
   exact conventions. They do not give a prefix-preserving injection
   from all two-coordinate contest states to canonical words at one
   common radix. No multiplicity contradiction has been proved.

7. **One sparse count contradicts the source family in D7C.** Missing.
   Sources can move among different difference lines, sizes and clocks;
   their growth tends to infinity. D6's fixed-line/common-translation
   power saving cannot be applied to this moving family without a new
   counting/transport theorem. Even infinitely many sparse sources
   would not alone contradict a sublinear count.

8. **A known scalar rounding theorem settles this map.** Unsupported.
   The actual primary scalar theorem contexts retain their specified
   rational slope, residue target and denominator conditions. They do
   not prove the missing full-map branching or source return theorem.
   No eventual periodicity/normality or rational-to-profinite-to-real
   identification has been assumed. A rational matrix digit paper was
   a discovery lead with body access failure, not an applied theorem.

The exact first-hit family is a small positive result about an actual
unbounded family. The principal new checkpoint is the proved-in-full
