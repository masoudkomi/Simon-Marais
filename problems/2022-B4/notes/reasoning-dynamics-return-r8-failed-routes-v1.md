unbounded hyperplane targets. The distinction matters even in the
official residue sector a=2u,b=3v. There the ACTUAL map is componentwise

the explicit problem definition, theorem statement and selected proof
passage were used to diagnose the absent interface. It is not a
mathematical premise of D8A--D8D.

## B. Canonical normality automatically transports to the coupled map

The current AEV primary introduction calls normality of EVERY specified

## E. Fixed growth, fixed depth, and full ancestry are interchangeable

They are different. D8A counts same-output, same-depth points and uses
that they are an antichain. Comparable even-a parity twins have equal
growth but never merge. Ancestors at different depths can have very
different sizes. Outside-strip ancestors need not satisfy width36.
The whole-ancestry estimate is obtained only AFTER aligning exact
growth-phase clocks, not by applying width8 directly to the union.

## F. The phase clock is free to shift to the original start

No. A depth-k ancestor of collateral z_n has phase n-1-k. It need not
reach clock0, and a negative phase is possible only down to -m. The
new bound shows that K_n>=epsilon*n occurs at O(log N) clocks through
N, not that all depths vanish or that there is a last exceptional
clock. The computable M bounds total negative initial levels only;
it is not an effective universal hitting time.
