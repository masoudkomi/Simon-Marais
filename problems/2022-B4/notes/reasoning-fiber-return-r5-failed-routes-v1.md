# Failed and conditional routes in this construction

2026-10-18 UTC. **Candidate proof - unverified.** No failure below is
silently promoted into a hypothesis. All actual examples are hand
identities, not an orbit scan or an infinite survivor.

1. **Same growth=>merger is false.** Even-a parity twins keep gap(1,0)
   forever, for arbitrary official size. Equal-growth rigidity must
   conclude a permanent translation, not equality of states. The actual
   (30,30)/(31,30) pair has different FIRST diagonal times0/1; neither
   example has unequal eventual-hitting truth values proved here.
2. **No unordered reversal is false.** The explicit official family
   (60L+29,60L+20),(60L+30,60L+19), L>=1, reverses gap(1,-1) to(-1,2).
   The correct proof allows that exact small reversal and uses parity
   to prevent a second one. An orientation axiom is not assumed away.
3. **Finite event count=>uniform last-event time is false.** CF4 has
   one separation change at time N+1 and first merger N+2, for arbitrarily
   large N while the initial gap is always(2,-3) and both d values are
   already in I. A size-dependent synchronization bound is not refuted;
   no such bound is proved either. The changing N states do not have
   one ordinary infinite limit, and their merged future remains unknown.
4. **Finite growth fibers=>effective membership is missing.** At one
   real C there are finitely many initial official I-states, but deciding
   which have EXACT Lambda=C is an all-time question. Eventual constant
   gaps may wait arbitrarily long before another change. The proof
   supplies neither an algorithm nor a pointwise stopping certificate
   for such a negative claim. Existence of a real limit is not its
   effective separation from another limit.
5. **All permanent translates pull back is missing.** A late rectangle
   gives honest ordinary forward trajectories from its own clock.
   It need not be the image of the full earlier I-fiber. Accepted Q
   births/orphans show why ordinary predecessors and inside-strip
   predecessors are distinct. No inverse-limit or all-parent claim is
   inserted into CF3. The new common-clock finite-cohort inclusion is
   deliberately one-sided.
6. **A canonical plateau gives many distinct contest expansions is an
   invalid interface.** The conditional CF6 rectangle is in the ordinary
   TWO-coordinate contest state space, not the prefix tree. Its sum
   growth is12G, and its336 distinct starts are not336 canonical words.
   A map preserving real value need not be injective or preserve every
   integer prefix. No cardinality contradiction to the full q-bound
   follows. This failed route is why CF6 is conditional geometry only.
7. **Generic smooth monotone dynamics is not an applied theorem.** The
   real floor extension is not the smooth injective map in the standard
   derivative criterion. On the lattice continuity alone is automatic
   and does not fix the orientation issue. Under the cone-switched
   interpretation, the actual reversal family prevents simply asserting
   the global order-reflection property. The submitted elementary
   floor/parity proof replaces that unsupported import.
8. **Proper-residue termination remains a conjectural bridge.** The
   original approximate-multiplication paper treats the two-residue
   p=q+1 problem as an open direction; singleton divisibility does not
   settle q10. Current normality implications are conditional. No
   universal no-tenfold theorem or every-positive-orbit residue theorem
   is supplied by the reads. This is not a claim that no applicable
   result exists anywhere in the literature.
9. **Controlling a tail's displacement is still necessary.** The new
   comparison theorem makes equal-growth ordinary quotient tails
   eventually identical. The surviving bounded coordinate translation
   changes diagonal contact. We did not prove that a general avoiding
   trajectory shares growth with a selected hitting one, or that its
   displacement is zero. Those are real unclosed arithmetic inferences.

The new unconditional partial results are the complete model-specific
comparison, growth rigidity, rectangles and actual delayed merger family.
They are not claims of a solved birth family, universal equality,
constructed survivor, no-tenfold theorem or priority. No larger table,
