# D8 constructor hand checks

Candidate proof - unverified. These are displayed mathematical checks,
not implementation tests, a computer certificate, or independent review.
The whole new argument needs TWO OTHER initially blind ENTIRE audits.

1. **Antichain endpoint.** A common first coordinate would order two
   states. After sorting, a rises at least2 and b drops at least3 by
   the residues of their COMMON output. Eight points need a difference
   spread at least35, consistent with width36; nine need40, impossible.
   No assertion that an eight-point inverse fiber actually occurs.
   The k0 fiber has one point and also satisfies the bound.
2. **Domain.** The antichain lemma works on all ordinary Z2, but the
   finite clock mass uses ONLY positive/product-legal strip ancestors.
   Their growth is positive. Negative auxiliary stationary points
   cannot be inserted into the mass sum or produce an infinite ray.
3. **Actual time indices.** A depth-k ancestor of z_n reaches x_n in
   k+1 steps. Its phase t=n-1-k reaches x_N in N-t steps. This is why
   there are N nonnegative levels0..N-1 and why the chosen source lag
   is k_n+1 rather than k_n.
4. **Unique levels.** The same physical ancestor cannot occur in two
   different A_n by the D7 properness argument, and cannot have two
   depths within one A_n because its positive growth would have to
   differ by a nonzero power of beta. Sum of cardinalities therefore
   has no accidental multiplicity before applying width8.
5. **Negative initial clocks.** Ancestors may predate x's clock0.
   Lambda>=1 gives the exact cutoff -m. The spine is only defined at
   nonnegative clocks; assigning seven slots to negative clocks would
   be unjustified. The proof uses eight for those m levels. Replacing
   m by M costs no algorithm for the exact real limit.
6. **Whole ancestry.** We first bound every ancestor, with no avoidance
   assumption. K_n+1 is bounded afterwards by choosing one longest
   real strip path. The estimate is not an assumption that all other
   ancestor paths are disjoint inside a single A_n.
7. **Dyadic constant.** In block[2^j,2^(j+1)), a K>=epsilon*n event
   costs at least epsilon*2^j. Cumulative mass at2^(j+1) is at most
   7*2^(j+1)+8m. Summing gives14/epsilon for EACH block and a geometric
   tail at most16m/epsilon. This deliberately bounds the last partial
   block by a longer horizon. It does not claim optimal constants.
8. **Density is actual output time.** If limsup C_N/N>delta, subtract
   at most(7N+8m)/(H+1) long-depth events. For H+1>7/delta a positive
   upper density remains. Neither positive density nor infinitely
   many sign crossings is assumed to be true for every orbit.
9. **Avoidance enters late.** For a never-hit orbit, diagonal input
   uniqueness excludes a diagonal collateral. An off-diagonal parent
   of an avoiding source automatically avoids at time0 and all later
   times, so it contradicts the maximum Omega depth. An earlier full
   ancestor that already hit is not counted as an avoiding source.
10. **Raw sources.** Distinct sources eventually leave the finite
    b<=42 patch. At b>=43 Q3's actual inequalities put EVERY ordinary
    parent in O. An inside parent must then be diagonal; otherwise
    there is no inside parent, Q's orphan case. This DOES NOT enforce
    the extra next-output rectangle exclusion in Sdagger.
11. **Crossing endpoints.** For d2, 10d_next>=-1 implies integer
    d_next>=0, rather than incorrectly permitting -1. For d1 and
    d_next<0, the only option is -1 and r-s=-19. With a=b+1,
    a divisible30 and b=19mod20 mean a divisible60. The t1 inputs
    (60,59)/(58,62) have products3540/3596. Both images are(64,65).
    These two hand points check the exact parameter endpoint; no
    orbit or residue program was run.
12. **Remaining branches.** An infinite avoider could be one-sided;
    could oscillate with zero-density crossings; or could generate
    fresh, large raw sources at bounded lag. No branch is excluded.
    The abstract comb is a limitation of the estimate, not a genuine
    ordinary trajectory. CF6 conditional existence is unused.
