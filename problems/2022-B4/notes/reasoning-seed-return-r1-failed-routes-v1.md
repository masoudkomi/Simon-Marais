# Failed and limited routes in the seed-return research

2026-10-18 UTC. **Candidate proof - unverified.** The main contains
complete proofs of the new scoped deductions. These are actual limits
encountered in obtaining them, not counterexamples to the official
question or claims that no further proof can exist.

1. **One constant-offset equivalence does not settle its existence.**
   The scalar digit restriction0..3 is not contradicted by the accepted
   at-most-ten full-canonical bound. It asks for at least eight, which
   that bound permits. The new explicit map to a difference1 contest
   orbit is a genuine ordinary interface, but its input orbit remains
   unknown. A varying-difference contest survivor need not produce this
   particular restricted scalar orbit by the proof given.

2. **Classical proper-residue termination is still a hypothesis.**
   The singleton divisibility proof works for a fixed one-entry residue
   interval. It does not iterate separately over each allowed residue:
   a path can change residues. No universal proper-residue termination,
   normality or at-most-two conjecture is used as proved. The source's
   conditional Mahler-number implication is not reversible into an
   ordinary contest construction.

3. **Central entry is not trapping or equality.** C=[-9,19] is not
   treated as invariant. For example d19,t40 gives d_next20 directly
   from(3.1); therefore after the proved orphan return a later exit
   remains possible. That example is a hand formula, not a search.
   For j>=1 the accepted rank at the orphan is1320j+47, whereas every
   subsequent state has Phi>=1452j+40, already larger. Thus the
   proved forward return is not Q8's required rank descent. A later
   smaller parent may just lead back to the same orphan. The exact
   entry time does not remove this family from the survivor problem.

4. **Outer monotonicity has a real hard tail.** A trajectory forever
   outside C would stabilize at an outer d, but its constant phase can
   allow TWO rank residues. In that case the argument gives the maximal
   tenfold fiber problem, not a contradiction. There is no uniform bound
   on phase waiting times just because at most three strict drops occur.
   SR3 works exactly where the lattice congruence leaves ONE rank.

5. **One arithmetic progression is an extra condition.** A ninefold
   fiber supplies some positive ordinary ceiling seed. SR4 requires the
   specific progression H=121j+122. F5's common radix shift does not
   place an arbitrary seed in that progression, nor preserve a desired
   tail phase backwards through a canonical fork. The accepted R8 fork
   obstruction already warns against this inference; it is not new here.

6. **Long constant phases are not infinite ordinary phases.** The born
   congruences have solutions for every finite N, but the solutions can
   differ. The limiting parameter -124/121 is not an allowed ordinary j.
   Its evaluated postimage(6,2) fails the product condition. Evaluating
   the initial affine family at that rational parameter also fails the
   assumed affine image formula: the actual floor map fixes
   (682/121,440/121), rather than mapping it to(6,2). This is an exact
   obstruction to the proposed interpolation, not an infinite positive
   contest counterexample.

7. **The fixed low-residue phases do have inadmissible fixed points.**
   For the difference1 construction, ceiling fixed states H=-3,-2,-1,0
   have corrections3,2,1,0. Their mapped b values are0,6,12,18, with
   a=b+1. None meets the official product/positivity hypotheses.
   The main uses H>0 and an actual ordinary future throughout; changing
   signs or shifting a small fixed point is not a proven infinite lift.

8. **A reflection does not preserve the target.** Directly, the affine
   map R(a,b)=(29-a,19-b) commutes with F, because the two reflected
   floor quotients are their negatives. It sends d to10-d, maps large
   positive states to negative ones, and sends equality to difference10.
   Thus it does not turn an outer-band or negative fixed point into an
   official avoiding start. No reflection theorem is a premise of the
   main. The calculation is recorded as an explored route only.

9. **Span closure and finite-code compactness are different.** The
   primary subtree paper's small-base result concerns the closure over
   all seeds. It supplies neither the required attained plateau at one
   positive ordinary seed nor a boundary-preserving conversion from
   arbitrary real expansions. No compactness argument fills that gap.

10. **No routine enlargement of data was substituted.** No residue
    table, orbit sample, CAS expansion, finite-prime scan, old certificate
    or author code was run. The only code in this packet hashes and
    validates immutable records/labels. It cannot validate any universal
    mathematical statement. The current genuine new positive inference
    is SR3's whole unbounded return, while SR1/SR2/SR4 retain their exact
    unresolved existence or future limits.
