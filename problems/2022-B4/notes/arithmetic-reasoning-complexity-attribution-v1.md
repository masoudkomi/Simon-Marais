## Published work identified after the candidate was frozen

Artūras Dubickas, *On integer sequences generated by linear maps*,
Glasgow Mathematical Journal **51** (2009), 243–252,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0017089508004655),
[publisher PDF](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/C40C0C07FEC20797475BB2899C436C9A/S0017089508004655a.pdf/on_integer_sequences_generated_by_linear_maps.pdf).

The original Theorem 3 on journal page 245 and its complete proof in
Section 4 on page 249 were read through the publisher's public PDF on
2026-10-16 UTC. For scalar iteration
\(x_{n+1}=\lceil px_n/q\rceil\), it gives the factor-complexity lower
slope \(\log q/\log(p/q)\) for \(qx_{n+1}-px_n\). Its proof compares
divisibility of the difference at two matching blocks with the orbit's
real growth. It also explains that this excludes Sturmian correction
words. The candidate's complexity mechanism should therefore be credited
as an adaptation of Dubickas's argument, rather than presented as a new
method.

The source was found through the introduction, equation (1.3), of
Andrieu, Eliahou and Vivion,
[*A Normality Conjecture on Rational Base Number Systems*,
arXiv:2510.11723v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.11723v2), 7 April 2026.
That source still presents full normality as conjectural.
