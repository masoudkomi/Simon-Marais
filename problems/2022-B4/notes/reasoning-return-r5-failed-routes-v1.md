## F3. A prefix value is not an infinite expansion

Several words in one real fiber can share a prefix. Injectivity is only
used for words of one finite length and their finite values. The proof
first bounds the number of infinite fiber words, then chooses a common
level after ALL of them have separated. At later levels the values
are consecutive because of ordered-level and continuation arguments.
One cannot infer the fiber size from one arbitrary early level.
