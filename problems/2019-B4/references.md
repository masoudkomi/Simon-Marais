# 2019 B4(b): references and published status

Checked 2026-10-16 through the primary-source web browser. Source URLs are
kept here; the coordinator owns shared source-cache manifests.

## Primary sources

- [Official 2019 Paper B](https://www.simonmarais.org/uploads/8/2/3/5/82358688/smmc-2019-paper-b_1.pdf),
  B4 on p. 2. The as-issued statement labels only (b) open.
- [Official 2019 solution notes](https://www.simonmarais.org/uploads/8/2/3/5/82358688/smmc-2019-solutions_1.pdf),
  B4 on pp. 10–12. These establish the published preliminary bounds with
  bases \(\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\) and the real root \(\psi>1\) of
  \(x^3-x^2-x-1\), but do not determine the requested limiting values.
- [Official 2019 archive](https://www.simonmarais.org/2019.html).
  Inspected for updated solution material; no separate later resolution of
  B4(b) was located there in this first audit.
- Anthony Henderson and S. Ole Warnaar,
  [“The 2019 Simon Marais Mathematics Competition,” Gazette of the Australian Mathematical Society 47(1), 2020, p. 39](https://austms.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/471Web.pdf).
  The organiser-authored article explicitly describes B4(b) as the unresolved
  asymptotic-enumeration problem at the time of that report. This is a
  historical status source, not proof that the problem remains open today.
