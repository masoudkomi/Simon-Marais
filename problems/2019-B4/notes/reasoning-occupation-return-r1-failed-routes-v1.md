# AU unsuccessful routes and exact remaining obstruction

2026-10-18 UTC. Candidate proof - unverified. Hand research only.

## F1. Small-angle counting cannot select the critical regime

A source-position subset of size a determines both chronological tapes,
so `M(a,N-a)<=binom(N,a)`. This bounds very uneven bidegrees by their
binary entropy and excludes angles close enough to +/-1 at full lambda
entropy. The first informal thought incorrectly treated the conditional
angular law as uniform on all[-1,1]. The accepted finite-critical law is
uniform only on[-v0,v0], where v0 is unknown, strictly between0 and1.
Its entire interval could avoid the excluded endpoint cones. Moreover
it is a random-length Boltzmann law, not a fixed-N local limit. Thus
the lead is recorded as insufficient, with no new regime conclusion.

## F2. Grid density and higher convolution do not close the gap

An elementary2x2 height-band argument can limit a raw matching grid's
density. It supplies no actual upper bound on full viable UNION mass,
and conditional constants alone would at most constrain unknown mu.
Likewise C^k counts compatible ordered decompositions, not arbitrary
tuples of vertices from the union over different histories. Substituting
V^k would lose compatibility. No theorem from either tentative route
is claimed in this checkpoint.

## F3. Two-dimensional flat vertices must have actual histories

CT's old one-layer shifts used f=-e. Simply drawing a rectangle around
each cut would be invalid. AU1 instead deletes the explicitly scheduled
pads on both sides and splits the central LL/RR loops independently.
This produces all rectangle points at possibly different times, with
actual suffix completions. The two disjointness estimates are essential;
counting histories or overlapping clusters would not prove the area bound.

## F4. One rare flat does not bound the full high-area class

The bare flat has only polynomially many degree-specific outputs, hence
is exponentially rare. That remains true and is not suppressed. AU2
injects arbitrary actual prefixes beside a fixed flat block; AU4 injects
two arbitrary actual contexts into an input-pair space. These prove lower
bounds on the enlarged full event. Neither construction describes all
outputs in that event or gives a mass upper bound.

## F5. Full entropy at a growing threshold is not typicality

For a_N=o(N²), the lower probability may be exp(-constant*sqrt(a_N))
times a polynomial. For residual u_T=o(T), it may be exp(-constant*u_T)
times a polynomial. Both have probability root1 and can tend to zero.
Neither establishes a positive limit, mean divergence, an actual
pressure cusp or either critical alternative. The desired fixed-positive
quadratic-density upper tails remain open; an eta-dependent exponential
loss is consistent with every new lower bound.

## F7. Potential future direction remains genuinely model-specific

A useful upper estimate must cover ALL positive quadratic viable-area
outputs, or all corresponding linear unconfined residual contacts,
including the multiscale fast/slow ladders. It must permit density-
dependent loss, account for phase-correct actual prefixes/suffixes and
respect the uniform-output versus input-size-biased distinction. Neither
raw occupation, fixed-range factor rarity, a short separator or a
general membership algorithm supplies this missing estimate. No proposed
cover, pressure regularity or actual parameter value is assumed here.
