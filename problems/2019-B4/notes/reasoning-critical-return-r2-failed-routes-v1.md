## 1. A raw-walk rarity argument is not enough

Uniform compatible tape pairs are not pairs of independent binary walks.
There are up to2^n raw words at total length n but only c_n actual contexts,
with rate lambda<2. A raw exponential rarity estimate must beat this
change of measure; it cannot simply be asserted to hold under the actual
uniform output law. The successful new strip bound instead cuts an ACTUAL
canonical witness, counts prefix/suffix outputs by c_p,c_q, and confines
the entire other word in the middle. This supplies a genuine entropy gap
without such an independence assumption.

## 8. Permutation capacity is a different count

The Körner--Malvenuto collision graph uses a position at which two
permutation values differ by exactly1. Different parity patterns do not
force this. For a hand example, take the two unimodal permutations

    (1,2,3,4,5,6,7),  (1,4,6,7,5,3,2).

The coordinate differences are0,2,3,3,0,-3,-5, so none is+1 or-1,

proposed converse, not to the source theorem. Thus the source's clique
