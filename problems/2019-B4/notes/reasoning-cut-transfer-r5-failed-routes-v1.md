# Failed inferences and retained limits — cut-transfer-r5

2026-10-18 UTC. **Candidate proof - unverified.** These are actual
reasoning routes considered, not hypotheses quietly added to the main.
No mathematical program or certificate replay was used.

## F1. A raw random-walk local-time estimate does not transfer

Many feasible antidiagonal cuts satisfy a height equality. Two independent
raw binary walks have familiar return statistics, but uniform actual
outputs have exponential growth lambda<2 and are a different measure.
A raw exponential or anti-concentration bound would need its exact
change of measure proved. No such transfer was obtained. Even a height
equality supplies neither admitted prefix nor admitted suffix.

## F2. Generic locations introduce a polynomial loss

The earlier CR proof of an arbitrary middle factor has both an actual
prefix and suffix, and their convolution incurs a polynomial in total
length. It cannot simply be reworded as a uniform bound for a sum over
all displaced contacts under independent inputs. The new one-ended
encoding removes one outside component, and the accepted ratio inequality
then pays the remaining actual prefix/suffix exactly. This is the new
positive step, not an optimized arbitrary-location CT estimate.

## F3. Large fixed range is not covered by the same geometric sum

The direct path bounds work because the other middle word's growth
sqrt3 is strictly below lambda. With more allowed height levels that
growth can approach 2. Nothing justifies summing the resulting geometric
series at the unknown actual lambda or using CT's all-range statement
with constants uniform in total length at a specified end. The new
result keeps ranges one/three and treats constant words separately.

## F4. Short end guards do not fix the old separator

Uniform exponential tails for constant end pieces make many endpoint
statistics tight, but this does not supply a recoverable seam. BT4's
entire counterfamily has maximum run ONE on all input tapes, and any
fixed first/last windows can be identical across an unbounded fibre.
Only the stated marker is refuted; no theorem excludes all separator
designs. The listed family's negligible input mass does not establish
average control for the full high-cut class.

## F5. Input bias must remain in the formula

Independent uniform actual inputs push forward with weight k, rather
than uniformly over outputs. The old uniform mean is A, whereas the
new input mean is E_o(k^2)/A. Bounded A alone need not control that
second moment. Nor can the J bound under input law be relabelled a
complete bound on the uniform-output cut number.

## F6. Tight J does not bound k

The estimate covers every alternative cut touching a tested end piece,
including an arbitrarily long constant piece. It does not prove that
all contacts are of that form. Accepted CT3/CN show why a fixed-range
deterministic cover cannot be assumed. BT3 instead states the necessary
large-range contact law that criticality would force. No contradiction
with that law was proved.

## F7. Endpoint-height and language-regularity leads remain incomplete

A possible route was anti-concentration for the heights of independent
input prefixes/suffixes; no such actual-measure theorem was proved.
The explicit finite vectors count only the confined middle words.
No general endpoint pressure, normal limit, full grammar, finite
presentation, normality of a height kernel or description of all
minimal bad factors is imported.

## F8. Published terminology is not a model theorem

The primary Arratia–Goldstein–Kochman passage supplies the standard
definition of size bias, with positive finite mean. Its concentration,
renewal, coupling and tightness results are not applied to this tape
model. The finite formulas here are proved directly. No unread source
