# A second-pass algebraic direction

Coordinator exploratory note, 2026-10-16. This is a proposed research representation and approach, not an accepted proof or an evaluation of the growth constant. The existing partial candidate remains frozen for its independent audits.

Reverse the final left context, so both tapes record chronological additions. Group the mandated source bits into pairs 01. The marked-context monoid is generated by four pairs under componentwise concatenation:

    A=(01,empty), B=(0,1), C=(1,0), D=(empty,01).

Every length-k product corresponds to a legal 2k-move history, and every marked context has such a product. The recoding should be checked explicitly before relying on it. Consequently C_k is the number of distinct degree-k elements in this concrete submonoid of {0,1}* times {0,1}*. The question becomes one of canonical forms and growth for this explicitly generated monoid.

Some readily checked pair equalities are

    AD=DA, BCB=ABD, CBC=DCA.

These encode occurrences of the original four-move relation at the two possible block phases; they are not being claimed as a complete presentation. The additional 12-move relation corresponds to

    A C B B C D = D B C C B A,

both of which yield chronological context pair (011001,011001). An exact normal-form/completion computation should retain actual tape pairs, so an inferred relation is always certified and absence of a relation in a finite run is not overinterpreted.

Possible next routes: a noncommutative monomial-ideal/canonical-form calculation, or a direct characterization of two strings whose shuffle is (01)^k. A finite completion can improve upper bounds; only a general completeness/confluence proof would justify an exact language or generating function. Do not assume that the displayed small relations are complete or that their avoidance language counts distinct pairs exactly. No external theorem is imported by this note.
