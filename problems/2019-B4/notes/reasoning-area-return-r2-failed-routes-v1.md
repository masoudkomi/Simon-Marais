# Genuine failed routes and the exact remaining interface

2026-10-18 UTC. These are research failures/limits, not unrestricted
impossibility theorems. The new proposed positive results are AR1--AR4
in the main. All old notes remain untouched.

1. **Independent unit-oscillation removal.** I considered replacing
010 by0 and101 by1 separately on the tapes to leave a simpler walk
signature. The actual discovery pair(010,10011) is reachable by
LRRLRRLR, but(0,10011) is not. AR3 strengthens this to actual balanced
outputs with V/N²→3/32. The right word in the final family is
1(0011)^k0. An exploratory concatenation(100110)^k was not already
irreducible: its joins contain101. That error was caught by hand
before writing the final claim, which uses the correct explicit run
word. The counterexample concerns THIS rewrite, not published coupled
signatures or all possible annotations.

2. **Treating every minimal bad history as an even balanced loop.**
The old accepted canonical note already has nonzero odd coefficients,
including f17=2. Therefore an assumed scalar obstruction series made
only of even balanced words is invalid. Those old finite data were
read as established context, not rerun or enlarged. A phase-sensitive
weighted renewal algebra may still be possible, but no new universal
charge classification or endpoint-moment theorem is proved here.

3. **Raw simple-walk local times.** Each canonical history can be
mapped to a nearest-neighbor height-difference walk, but the uniform
DISTINCT output law is uniform on canonical histories, not all2^N
histories. A raw estimate of the form2^N exp(-I(eta)N), divided by the
known c_N>=lambda^N, is ineffective as eta decreases because I(eta)
can tend to zero while log(2/lambda)>0. No independence assumption is
available from this map.

4. **Occupation forces many confined pieces, then CT handles them.**
Linear occupation indeed creates many short return intervals, but the
range needed to include length-T excursions can grow with T. Accepted
CT is fixed-range and its constants deteriorate with that range.
Accepted AU supplies full quadratic area with no arbitrarily long
factors at ANY separately prescribed fixed range. It refutes that
unqualified pointwise detector. A uniform exceptional-set count for
all high-area outputs is not supplied by those fixed-range bounds.

5. **Insert markers at every return and count subsets.** Inserting
identical alternating loops can merge into a single long01 power and
lose the chosen insertion positions. Taller markers require boundary
data and can have linear length cost. No injective gain covering the
full high-occupation class was established. Counting all insertions
or all histories as different outputs would be invalid.

6. **Balanced-block factorization automatically gives an entropy gap.**
AR4 proves actual blocks, including their phase. Their cut coordinates
and choices are not free recoverable information. Right-empty blocks
occur even for actual quadratic-area flats: the canonical path for
((01)^a,(01)^b) reads all left letters before all right letters.
Thus a claimed positive density of two-nonempty-tape blocks on every
canonical history would be false. No full-language block encoding,
independence law or renewal closure is inferred.

7. **Generic non-overlap axioms choose the regime.** The coordinator's
UNVERIFIED critical-code countermodel was read as failed-route context:
the different forbidden family RR(LR)^kLL has a critical double-pole
renewal series. It is not the actual F. Regardless of that note's
unverified status, no generic non-overlap-to-positive-defect inference
is used here. The source alone supplies no new actual-language theorem.

8. **Apply the exact subclass answer to the full law.** AR2's conditional
uniform distribution is an actual theorem proposal, but its whole
subclass has at most2^(m+1)-1 outputs at degree2m+delta and is
exponentially rare in full R_N. It does not cover AU's large-range
periodic flats. Thus its linear mean area and exponential quadratic
tail do not prove E_RN V=o(N²) or select positive defect/infinite mean.

The precise bridge suggested by the successful calculation is to find
an actual JOINT clock expenditure for more general tapes, retain the
order/phase information lost by independent simplification, and prove
an output-recoverable encoding in which its unused budget has a small
tail. No such unrestricted budget or encoding has been established.
That is a genuine missing inference, not a conditional restatement
being promoted as a solution. The present checkpoint contains a fully
scoped exact mechanism and an actual high-area counterexample.
