# 2017 B4 proof register

No candidate full solution or new substantive partial theorem has been proposed at the 2026-10-16 first-pass checkpoint.

Every future proof begins as **candidate proof - unverified**. Distinguish published reproductions from original results. Do not relabel until two independent adversarial audits, a reconstruction and all repairs are complete.

The inequalities in references.md are **attributed published theorems**, not results proved or independently reviewed by this project. The scripts provide finite computational evidence and check exact rational arithmetic; they do not prove the unrestricted asymptotic statements.

Potential future proof dependencies that need explicit audit:

- Faithfulness of the official tiling-to-marked-arrangement dictionary, including the geometric meaning of a tiling and the boundary orientation convention.
- Correctness and injectivity of any patch-gluing map; fixed local boundary pairing must be preserved.
- Correctness of any count recurrence, including floors, unused strands, and the passage from a subsequence to all sufficiently large n.
- A certified optimization bound, rather than a floating-point local maximum, if using a cutpath upper estimate.
- A complete quadratic-scale replication argument if attempting to prove existence of the limit. Ordinary one-dimensional superadditivity is insufficient.
