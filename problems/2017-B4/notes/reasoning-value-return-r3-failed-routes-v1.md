## 1. Independent placement lists lose real order constraints

The accepted V/OP products are sound UPPER relaxations. They do not make
the separate + contact allocations independent in actual tilings.
For one internal three-strand class 121, its heap is a three-event chain.
At a=1, a weak isotone level assignment has four possibilities, not the
six obtained from independent lists for x_1=2,x_2=1. In particular levels
0,1,0 in chronological event order are impossible: their proposed
level-zero events cannot be moved together past the noncommuting middle
event. The other internal class 212 has the same chain count. Thus the
two-class exact capacity is 8 and the old independent-list weight is 12.
These are hand-derived formulas illustrating the missing relation, not
a numerical search supplying an infinite conclusion.

HC1 retains the entire heap. HC2 then deliberately relaxes only the
comparabilities between a specified chain partition. The new scalar
inequality follows from that legitimate relaxation. It does not assume
that all of its records are actual.

## 2. A one-bundle realization does not solve general coarsening fibers

The staged bubble proof uses an external PARALLEL bundle. All A ranks
have the same ordered external list and all prefix sets match. With
several external colors their orders can differ. The accepted OP
example with m=2 and outside singleton colors B,C has exactly two actual
contact placements while the uniform one-bundle capacity H_2(2) is three.
This example remains unchanged. No assertion that same gap counts or
necessary prefix-set equalities suffice for arbitrary Q is made.

A sharper useful target is to count isotone assignments which ALSO pass
the actual Q-dependent prefix-set and complete realization constraints,
jointly over Q and A. HC1 gives an upper capacity, not that distribution.

## 3. Finite strictness does not give an extensive entropy saving

For every m>=4,a>0 the chain product admits an assignment violating a
cross-chain relation, so H_m(a)<T_m(a). This establishes a genuine finite
strict inequality only. It does not prove that log(T/H) is bounded below
by a positive multiple of m^2 along a proportional to m. Nor does a
single class with a large or small weight determine either weighted SUM.
The new global error is derived from the smaller slot count directly,
without such a mass assertion or the old brickwall/Jensen objection.

## 4. A known order-polytope leading term has the wrong uniformity

For a FIXED finite poset P with K events, its order polynomial has the
classical leading coefficient e(P)/K!. That is not uniform asymptotics
when both the internal class size and a grow, here K~m^2 and a~m at
one fixed D. Substituting this fixed-poset leading term into the
direction limit would exchange two uncontrolled limits.

Moreover summing e(P_A) counts individual representative words, which
are not the official C(m). Even where a classical sum of leading
coefficients has a word interpretation, it is not a replacement for
the complete finite weighted count in HC1. No word count or hook-length
formula is used in any HC proof.

## 5. Qualitative global recovery does not give an effective value

I examined whether the accepted compactness/profile/Vitali argument
could directly produce a finite certified side-length threshold. Its
pointwise differentiability windows, limiting common profile and
uniform positive-deficit contradiction currently provide no algorithm
for upper and lower approximation at a chosen fixed D. HC3 improves
the outer D error but does not change that interface.

The accepted V3 E/R hypotheses and V4 abstract obstruction were read in
full. They show exactly why existence of finite counts and a qualitative
limit is not the needed effective certificate. V4 is not an actual
tiling noncomputability result, and I make no such claim.

## 6. Free entropy and insertion estimates remain unmatched interfaces

A finite-alphabet free-boundary or mixing entropy theorem would need an
exact model identification and both comparisons with the prescribed
ranked zonogon boundary. Those hypotheses have not been supplied. The
published partition framework itself distinguishes boundary conditions;
an entropy density for unconstrained plane patches cannot replace ell_D.

I also considered deriving a uniform ray-entropy continuity estimate
from a simple-arrangement cutpath bound. A parallel-color insertion
has prescribed ports and forbids crossings with its own color. The
inspected simple marked-arrangement cutpath discussion does not by
itself establish the exact completion/injection required for that
model. No such bound or new ray-limit theorem is claimed. The finite
heap capacity avoids needing this missing reduction.
