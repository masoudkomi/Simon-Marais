# PUS attempted interfaces and retained limits

2026-10-18 UTC. Candidate proof - unverified; no annual/value claim.

1. Directly invoking the full US lower theorem on a plane graph would
   omit its full-tiling input. The written proof completes a finite patch
   containing the ENTIRE buffer first, then applies that accepted theorem.
2. Completing the upper family's largest boundary class does not ensure
   that a separately specified reference T belongs to it. No such assertion
   is made. Upper and lower are applied separately with the SAME rational
   receiving frame/phase; absolute offsets are handled by its established
   rounding formula, which does not change C_m.
3. Leaving the completed exterior fixed would not prove the original
   plane-reference statement. The exact(P1) two-way boundary-gluing map
   discards that exterior and keeps T^(M) outside the original M Omega_T.
4. A translation x_out at coarse scale becomes M x_out after dilation;
   its lift counterpart becomes M b. Neglecting this would mismatch every
   boundary rank and physical seam. Both maps are written with these factors.
5. One bounded-content completion is not a negligible collar or density-one
   packing. None of its area/angle constants is used in the lower count.
   The strict local theorem still leaves global mass and boundary strata.
6. A finite local pattern may have no plane representative. The proof starts
   with actual plane tilings and never claims finite SFT extension.
7. The arbitrary inserted interiors need not stay epsilon-near an affine
   lift. The old O(L) profile control is retained; the stronger estimate
   cannot be asserted merely from the boundary condition.
8. No exact scalar, improved numerical enclosure, new reference-existence
   theorem or computability conclusion has been obtained. The new claim is
   the precise local lower/recovery interface, conditional on the fully
   stated actual strict references at the scale under consideration.

No counterexample to the written interface was found by the constructor's
hand reasoning. This is not an independent review. Both OTHER whole audits
