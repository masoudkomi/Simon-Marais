# Actual failed and insufficient routes before R11

Candidate proof - unverified. These limits are part of the whole packet.

* A common pointwise correction from uniform reversal words to classes
  cannot supply the needed quadratic entropy, by accepted R10's actual
  singleton/layered families. Its large-scale pressure erases the desired
  term. R11 therefore never uses W_n, m(A) or their pushforward law.
* Merely replace PM8 kappa by twice itself: this misses both the extreme
  second-edge probability and the stronger degree-two bottom loss. It
  can give an incremental moment, but is not the complete new proof.
* Apply flow to the new raw weights: false because sigma2=chi>1.
  Any claim to have improved WO's feasible-family optimum that way
  would omit a cost. The full normalization/discount/cancellation pays it.
* Select one bounded second-step route when the intermediate degree is
  huge: no uniform route probability follows. R11 instead pays success
  already at that intermediate vertex and sums its whole row to one.
* Ignore shared endpoints of adjacent trials: targets, not starts, are
  the disjoint sets. Counting an endpoint twice could invalidate the
  full-product comparison. The exact greedy targets are specified.
* Prove only G<K: insufficient since the current accepted base is H<K.
  The derivative/rational comparison against EXACT H is indispensable.
* Use the new weighted class moment as a probability under uniform
  classes, or as a variational formula for the scalar: not justified.
  It is an absolute finite sum. No attaining family or rate is supplied.
* Maximize over all possible deletion orders and divide by their count:
  no uniform multiplicity identity is available. Only one canonical
  order is used, without a quotient or extra factorial.

Surviving mathematical gap: determine the common scalar value or
obtain a matching lower family / effective upper approximation. R11's
fixed explicit upper improvement and pressure estimate do not decide
those questions. No unread source, computation or claimed typical
geometry fills this gap.
