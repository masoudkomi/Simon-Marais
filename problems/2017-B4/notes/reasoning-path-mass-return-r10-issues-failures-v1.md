## Proof boundaries retained in the candidate

N01 — CLOSED BY DOMAIN. Auxiliary empty words w_0,w_1 do not extend the
official n>=2 domain. The n=2 matching has an empty even layer, and 0!=1.
The asymptotic lower estimate uses n>=4 exactly; the finite upper bounds
and minimax/measure definitions include n=2,3 without that lower estimate.

N02 — CLOSED BY QUOTIENT. The singleton construction has no adjacent
commuting pair, so no commutation path can leave it. The existence of braid
moves is irrelevant to this quotient. Including braids would identify
different official tilings and invalidate the argument, so none is allowed.

N03 — CLOSED BY LOWER-BOUND DIRECTION. The product of factorials only counts
within-layer permutations. Further cross-layer commutations may increase
the same class. The proof never claims this product equals m(O_n) or m_max.

N04 — CLOSED BY DISTINCT-WORD RECOVERY. Known layer lengths recover the
chosen order in each segment. Repeated use of a generator in another segment
does not collapse two distinct words in this count. Words, classes and
boundary labels remain different objects.

N05 — CLOSED BY FINITE MEASURE FORMULA. Q is the pushforward of uniform
words, and U is uniform on classes. The singleton likelihood ratio is W/C,
not C/W. Both laws have the same finite support. The explicit rare-event
comparison alone proves neither noncontiguity nor a total-variation lower
bound nor distinct typical shapes.

N06 — CLOSED BY SCALING. The fully evaluated pressure in R10D is normalized
by n² ln n, whereas the official entropy is normalized by n². The leading
kink therefore erases the desired coefficient. The candidate makes no
derivative-limit interchange, no finite-alpha pressure limit and no claim
that the sufficient o(1/ln n) scale is necessary for every sequence.

N07 — CLOSED BY ACCEPTED INTERFACE. PM9C is used with its exact fixed H and
all-size N-1 exponent. The final L comparison uses the accepted scalar
existence scope. Historical conditional labels in preserved old suffixes
are not current authority. Older B construction/review roles do not provide
a fresh independent certification of those inputs.

N08 — OPEN MAIN GAP. No control of the actual class-multiplicity distribution
at its remaining n² scale is proved. Neither Q-leading scheduling entropy
nor two extremal classes supplies a typical U-family estimate. No matching
upper/lower value or improved official bound follows from this checkpoint.

## Attempted routes and why they do not finish the target

F01 — Uniform correction from sorting histories. REJECTED by the actual
families, rather than by an abstract model. Their log multiplicity spread
is asymptotic to n² ln n/2. Even the best common normalization has worst
log error asymptotic to n² ln n/4. This refutes a uniform exp(O(n²))
history-to-class comparison. It does not refute the true aggregate identity
C=W/(W/C), whose average denominator remains unknown.

F02 — Use random sorting-network limit shapes as uniform-class results.
NOT JUSTIFIED. Original AHRV and Dauvergne laws are uniform on words.
Their quotient measure is Q=m/W. Moreover, a timed trajectory need not be
class-invariant: exchanging disjoint first swaps (1,3) and(3,1) changes
the first intermediate permutation. No source theorem establishes the
missing uniform-class typical-multiplicity control here. No published
random-network theorem is refuted.

F03 — Obtain L from a fixed-temperature pressure. FAILS AT THIS SCALE.
The candidate evaluates ln Z/(n² ln n) exactly as max(tau,0)/2, but this
contains no nonzero value at tau=0. A parameter tending0 is not automatically
safe either: alpha/ln n with alpha>ln H already has a strictly excessive
quadratic liminf. The finite-alpha limit and the official quadratic
remainder remain undetermined. This is not an effective characterization
or a claim of a new thermodynamic model theorem.

F04 — Simultaneously insert several new strands and multiply cutpath
counts. UNPROVED. Separate single-strand cutpaths need compatibility and
cannot simply be multiplied. A series of binary-choice diamonds has 4^m
ordered pairs of paths but only 3^m pointwise ordered pairs; this is an
ABSTRACT warning about possible exponential compatibility loss, not a
constructed actual pseudoline arrangement or a theorem disproving every
multi-strand approach. No realization or general-D estimate is supplied.

F05 — Replace a worst-case cutpath bound by prevalence or an average-zone
estimate. UNPROVED. The published high-cutpath construction establishes
existence of certain parents; existence does not establish a sufficiently
large population for a lower entropy argument. The primary average/small
zone proposals were read as proposals, not imported as theorems. A local
straight-line strip construction was considered as intuition for many
faces touching a chosen pair, but it was not formalized into a global
average-zone statement or claimed as a counterexample to that conjecture.

F06 — Use the layered class as an official lower bound. INVALID. All the
factorially many words in its proof belong to ONE official tiling. It gives
no quadratic lower class count. The singleton construction likewise says
nothing about the total number or typical frequency of singleton classes.
No enumeration/classification of those classes was attempted.

F07 — Convert finite exact recognition or old lower certificates into an
upper convergence modulus. NOT ESTABLISHED. Accepted earlier V/R6/R7
results explicitly separate finite recognition, free windows, balanced
counts and effective upper R. The new finite sums and inequalities do
not discharge that separation. No new upper R or computability conclusion.

F08 — Claim novelty from a bounded source search. NOT ESTABLISHED. The
layered schedule is standard and the rigid words/scheduling interpretation
are elementary established objects. The proof is self-contained at its
new finite interfaces, but priority of its consequences has not been
established. A source word search is not a novelty certificate.
