# Upper-entropy source follow-up: two attractive shortcuts remain conditional

2026-10-17 UTC. Root research record, not a promoted theorem or a proof
of upper R. No new mathematical computation or paper-byte download.

[Hutchinson–Widom, Enumeration of octagonal tilings](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1306.5977v2),
v2 header16March2015, was opened through the primary arXiv PDF reader.
Actual read: title/definitions, sections1–2 and the complete discussion
and conclusions on PDF14–15, including Hypotheses17/18/20 and
Proposition19. These concern D4 fixed-boundary octagons. The advertised
two-sided entropy interval is CONDITIONAL on unproved monotonicity
hypotheses; the conclusion expressly leaves their proofs open. It is
not an unconditional convergence certificate and supplies no all-D
upper R. The enumeration algorithm was not executed, the numerical
tables were not independently verified, and the rest of its algorithmic
proof was not audited here. The record preserves a useful research
direction: a proved descent-coefficient monotonicity or suitable weaker
finite upper envelope could matter. No such proof is supplied here.

[Balister–Bollobás–Quas, Entropy along convex shapes, random tilings and
shifts of finite type](https://web.uvic.ca/~aquas/papers/paper15.pdf),
original-author posted15-page text, was opened. Actual read: definitions,
TheoremsA/B/2.1, Lemmas2.2–2.5 and their displayed proofs, and beginning
of2.1 proof on PDF1–6. TheoremA counts restrictions of GLOBAL shift
configurations to convex windows. TheoremB requires strong
irreducibility with a uniform filling length. These are not prescribed
zonogon-boundary counts. No complete remaining2.1 or TheoremB proof
read is claimed, and neither theorem is imported to fill the missing
project interface. The local tiling alphabet alone establishes neither
the required uniform filling property nor equality of these window
counts with B_D(p).

Searches used primary-oriented combinations of rhombus tilings, higher
codimension, variational principle, boundary, local entropy and zonogon.
Secondary results were discovery pointers only. The author institutional
record confirms the latter article's bibliography: Illinois J.Math.46
(2002),781–795, DOI10.1215/ijm/1258130984. No journal-PDF access, external
contact, paid service, access bypass or full-source redistribution was
used. Absence of a matching theorem in this bounded search is not a
novelty assertion or a claim that no such theorem exists.

Our own exact full-profile feasibility and the entropy-MASS assembly
question remain distinct. Neither of these sources licenses inferring
exponentially many compatible fillings from one admissible profile.
