# R6 actual failed routes and unresolved inference

2026-10-17 UTC. Candidate proof - unverified. This records real
reasoning, including ideas rejected before the final four claims.

1. **Heap strictness as a mass estimate.** The accepted H<T finite
   comparison exposes between-chain comparabilities. I considered a
   uniform quadratic loss by counting many independent violations,
   but did not prove the required independence or a weighted-SUM
   estimate. Correlations of isotone chain heights cannot be ignored.
   A speculative Schur/staircase correspondence had no established
   bijection and is not imported. No table or scan was used to select
   a favorable internal class.
2. **A bare finite local model.** Root's older integer-grid suggestion
   was a lead, not a theorem. PM1 actually compiles and decodes the
   local tile rules, proves proper once-only tracks, and normalizes
   finite upper counts. Merely saying “SFT entropy exists” would have
   skipped every one of these obligations.
3. **Generic incomplete diagrams.** It was tempting to apply full
   directed-geodesic or annular theorems to incomplete plane windows.
   That hypothesis was absent. PM2 instead starts with an ACTUAL plane
   tiling, proves a finite full completion, then PM3 uses the accepted
   full theorem. Arbitrary locally admissible finite blocks are not
   proved plane-extendable by this argument, nor are necessary prefix
   tests sufficient for an arbitrary decoration.
4. **Repeated track angles.** Directly quoting the distinct-angle step
   in de Tilière while merging parallel tracks could hide collapsed
   tiles/ranks. The main now gives an actual unit reembedding, countably
   generic perturbation, fixed finite-class subsequence and accepted
   consecutive-color DC smoothing. Positive old patch tiles survive
   with their exact group-summed cochain coordinates.
5. **A patch exterior of negligible cost.** Track preservation gives
   contents O(perimeter), hence an outer diameter O(perimeter), but
   this need not be a relative o(L) collar around a span-L patch.
   Nothing permits replacing that constant-factor area by density one.
   The periodic extension theorem in the same source is not a
   density-preservation theorem and was not used for that purpose.
6. **Free upper entropy equals balanced entropy.** PM4 is an actual
   counterexample already at D3: its exact positive gap follows from
   2^13>3^8. The periodic plane witnesses even approach the same tile
   densities as balanced zonogons. This defeats the claimed interface
   after FULL local encoding/decoding, rather than only criticizing an
   unproved formal model.
7. **Exact finite composition.** A pressure derivative and exponential
   tilt show concentration and provide approximately balanced finite
   sectors. They do not establish an exact1/3 sector at every torus
   size. The main and root update explicitly retain the LIMITING
   density scope. No exact-sector theorem is imported from an abstract
   or assumed just because three orientations are symmetric.
8. **Interchanging band and window limits.** For a fixed strict J,
   increasingly wide bounded-deviation plane classes may contain
   every finite pattern after enough padding. This does not justify
   exchanging the band supremum with the box-entropy infimum.
   PM3 requires whole-buffer near-flatness and retains only an upper
   receiving comparison, not a convergence rate for its entropy.

The useful remaining target is a finite count-MASS functional retaining
the ENTIRE prescribed macroscopic boundary/profile, with an actual
matching upper/lower realization theorem. GR realizes references and
PM2 supplies exact exteriors; neither alone supplies that mass theorem.
PM1's true finite certificates are deliberately not labeled hypothesis R.
No argument here proves L computable, noncomputable, or numerically
evaluated. No annual closure or literature priority claim is made.
