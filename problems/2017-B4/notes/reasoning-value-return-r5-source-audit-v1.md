## Primary analytic source actually opened

Leon Simon, *Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory*, author-posted
2017/2018 text, [Stanford PDF](https://math.stanford.edu/~lms/ntu-gmt-text.pdf).
The retrieved web document has 137 PDF pages, many two-page spreads.
The actual title/copyright and contents were read. In Chapter 2:

* Theorem 1.2, printed p.44, is scalar Lipschitz extension from a subset
  of a metric space. Its displayed envelope and proof were read.
* Theorem 1.4, printed pp.44--46, is Rademacher differentiability for
  Lipschitz scalar functions on Euclidean space. The statement and its
  operative Morrey proof through the countable direction argument were
  read, not just a search snippet. Apply it coordinatewise after scalar
  extension, or locally inside the open polygon.
* Theorem 3.3, printed pp.57--60, is the area formula for a locally
  Lipschitz map on an OPEN subset of R^n into R^m, n<=m, on measurable
  subsets, with its nonnegative Jacobian and multiplicity. The full
  relevant statement, remarks, and proof's positive-rank, zero-rank and
  nondifferentiability cases were read. The use here is n=m=2 on
  interior Omega and on the differentiable zero-determinant set.

The proof needs only that the critical image has planar measure zero.
The remaining null-image assertion is also reconstructed by Lipschitz
square coverings. No smooth Sard theorem is imported. No nonsmooth
inverse-function theorem is imported. A differentiability point with an
invertible derivative gives isolation of that ONE fiber and its local
winding by an explicit homotopy; it is not asserted to make the whole
map a neighborhood homeomorphism.

including additional opens filling the middle portions. Theorem numbering
and printed pages were checked against the actual PDF text. Web return
identities include turn1477view0--2, turn1478view0--2 and turn1479view0--2;
these are operational retrieval identifiers, not scholarly citations.

vectors, de Bruijn ribbons and the repeated-direction pseudoline bundles

introductory context was also read. Its later distance theorems are not
premises here, and the full later proof was not read for this packet.

The source uses unit vectors. Our integer vi=(1,i-1) deformation and its
exact marked count dictionary are an accepted separate internal result.

proof of arbitrary nonlinear-profile realization or of our dual embedding.
No mixing rate, entropy theorem or straightening converse is imported.
