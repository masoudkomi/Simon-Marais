Rejected. A computable sequence can converge to a noncomputable real.
V4 strengthens this observation to integer quadratic counts with exact
replication and identically zero balanced deficit. This does not settle
what additional tiling geometry can imply.

**F2: effective UR_D envelope alone computes k_D.**
Rejected as stated. Its rearrangement is a_D(r)-epsilon(D,r)<=k_D,
a lower bound. It supplies no upper approximation. V3 keeps (R)
separate. V4 has the analogous envelope zero yet a noncomputable limit.

**F3: a finite observed B_D(p) is automatically an upper bound for k_D.**
Rejected. G2 proves convergence without a sign for its finite errors;
even a one-sided deficit bound in (12) does not give that direction
of inequality. No numerical finite count is evaluated here.

**F4: generic SFT entropy theorem applies immediately to tilings.**
Not established. A finite-alphabet coding would need an exact entropy
identity with the prescribed full zonogon boundaries and normalization.
Strong irreducibility would require actual uniform separated-pattern
extension. Neither hypothesis follows from the word “tiling” or from
the older strict one-family replication. The published source's right
semicomputation and strong-mixing statements remain model-specific.

**F5: choose D large with n and use the balanced limit uniformly.**
Rejected. G2 is for each fixed D. The explicit outer error is useful
only after obtaining a justified approximation of the chosen ell_D.
V2 supplies no uniform p threshold.

**F6: all code choices must be liftable.**
Unnecessary and unsupported. V1 is an injection on actual diagrams.
Invalid choices only overcount. No arbitrary coarse insertion through
defects, density-one zonogon packing or quadratic full-B replication
is assumed.

**F7: the smoothed rank means the same original labelled strand.**
Rejected. Original labels exchange rank at each restored internal
contact. The invariant object is a rank track; its port list is
obtained from the restricted internal word and reconstructs label
routing only afterwards. Confusing labels with ranks would invalidate
the code.

**F8: concavify the necessary profile set or assert a variational value.**
Not supplied. The accepted structural K_D is necessary and can be
nonconvex. The new packet does not realize arbitrary profiles, take
a convex envelope as entropy, or replace the fixed boundary theorem
by a physics/free-boundary heuristic.

**F9: sharpen (5) by assuming that every mean-maximizing degree profile
is attained, or that it is far from attained.**
Neither assertion is proved or needed. The finite W_m(a) keeps the
realized degree-profile distribution. Estimating that distribution is

arithmetic mean constraint.
