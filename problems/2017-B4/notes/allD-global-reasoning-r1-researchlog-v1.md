## Routes rejected and limits retained

I did not use G4 acceptance as an all-D theorem or rename its dimension
without reconstructing the topology, codes and quantitative budgets.
The output of SP is the exact lower GE class, not a periodic family or
a balanced count substituted for ragged-boundary recovery. H_(D-1)
alone would not justify the needed local deficit.

Merging every tangent parallel group simultaneously would exceed the
accepted one-nonsingleton barrier theorem. The proof merges just one
adjacent pair. Zero rows are handled by such a pair, not by silently
assuming a general local zero-row deletion theorem. The earlier
single-erased-event lifting obstruction remains relevant and unchanged.
Closed-face incidence, not just a clean disk interior, is required.

The global-content M2 bound cannot be used when p is much larger
than a local side; the exact LW local code avoids that substitution.
Rare events may occur throughout a window. A low density does not
put them into a narrow band. The L+1 rare-count term and r0²L
code term are paid before the local scale limit.

One r0² deficit per block, without aggregation, would fail with
many tiny blocks in a face. The candidate uses the actual aggregate
perimeter and threshold split. Absolute heights are not free: one
O(log p) anchor per boundary window remains, and all per-window
fringe/component/perimeter terms are summed at the fixed finite list.

The limiting boundary need not be Jordan. It is not inserted into
a published smooth-boundary entropy theorem. Winding and bounded
convergence handle its domain directly; every finite disk still has
one actual Jordan boundary. The Vitali proof uses regular centered
parallelograms only after the compact strict truncation/finite frames.

Independently maximal code classes may have no common reference.
The proof conditions jointly before choosing T_j. A newly filled
target patch has a different interior inverse map; exact full
inverse/Jordan boundary control fixes its old fine region anyway.
Several global chart maps need not commute, because the fine
replacement patches with their fixed boundaries are what are glued.

Lower insertions may change upper profile/code constraints in collars.
That is allowed: the lower count is unrestricted Z_D(MB_j).
Arbitrary inserted interiors are not flat. Only complete fine
boundaries and the one full exterior are fixed.

An M threshold uniform across infinitely many original j is not
assumed. The finite product is formed at fixed j, then M grows.
RP uses ALL sufficiently large multiples of one fixed r; one finite
tiling or a favorable sparse subsequence cannot provide entropy.
Letting D grow happens only after each fixed-D limit inequality;
no rate uniform in D or rho->0 is used.

The official common value is not determined. This is a candidate
scalar-existence/equality partial result, not a full numerical
answer to2017B4, not an assertion of priority, and not PR.
