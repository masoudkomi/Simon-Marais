## 2. The fixed canonical frame and its exact entropy

Strict ordered row minors imply the row directions lie, in their
given order, in an open angle interval of width less than pi.
Thus their common positive cone has interior. Rationality permits
two independent rational u,v in it, with positive determinant;
common integer scaling makes Ju=a and Jv=b positive integral.
Since VJ=I, u=Va and v=Vb. The accepted A4 event words U,W
have exactly those contents, one fixed generic phase, and
U^mW^m<=W^mU^m for every integer m.

The boundaries are uniformly O_J(1) close, both physically and
in height, to the corresponding parallelogram and affine lift.
Deleting only bounded initial/final lobes gives a canonical
Jordan main lens. Because the lobe geometry and possible
tile records are uniformly bounded, its count M_m obeys

    C_m/B_J <= M_m <= C_m

for a fixed finite B_J>=1. Indeed the whole weak-lens count
factors over its fixed lobes, and each discarded lobe has
a fixed finite number of fillings. Appending one fixed
filling in every discarded lobe injects main-lens tilings
into the whole weak-lens family.

I checked the A5 entropy interface rather than substituting a
free-window theorem. For fixed l, block swaps give disjoint
actual size-l lenses with exactly matching ranked seams, plus
fixed fillings for mixed remainders. They give
C_m>=C_l^(floor(m/l)^2) for every integer m.
The finite local alphabet bounds log2 C_m by O_J(m^2).
Therefore s=lim m^-2 log2 C_m exists, is finite and nonnegative,
and equals the supremum of those finite-frame normalized logs.
The main lenses have the same s by the bounded factor above.
This is the one receiving frame family throughout ST1--ST3.

## 3. Shape separation and one common rounded offset

Let S be the matrix with columns u,v. Concentric enlargement
from side parameter L to L+2t adds the same fixed symmetric
parallelogram tS[-1,1]^2. Its distance between corresponding
supporting lines is at least c_shape t, where c_shape>0
depends only on S. The same margin holds for shrinkage.
This is a property of the matching shape; it does not follow
for arbitrary nearly affine Jordan domains of a different shape.

The desired corner of a centered frame of side m is
y=x0+(L-m)(u+v)/2. Round Jy+c once to an integer vector z.
Because VJ=I and Vc=0,

    Vz=y+O_V(1), (I-JV)z=c+O_J(1).

Thus translating the physical frame by Vz and its lift by z
preserves its bounded affine error relative to Jx+c,
without changing its phase words or assigning a new corner offset.
For a fixed L and source family, m,z and the phase are common
to all members. In insertion the inner frame is likewise fixed
before its fillings vary. Bounded main-lens trims only change
these estimates by O_J(1).

The canonical digital boundaries are x-monotone graphs with
bounded slopes, as are the source CUT boundaries. The fixed
geometric shape margin, minus the CUT band and bounded
rounding/trim error, therefore supplies genuine containment
with a vertex-to-boundary distance, not merely a Hausdorff
distance between two potentially crossing curves. Equivalently,
the canonical boundary can be deformed to its straight boundary
within its bounded band without crossing any point in the
deeply nested target. Both viewpoints give the needed nesting.

Choose A larger than a fixed multiple of the CUT band constant
divided by c_shape, with room for all bounded errors. Then for
small epsilon and large L, both the enlarged outer frame and
the inset inner frame have buffer at least

    t_buffer >= c4 A sqrt(epsilon)L

for a fixed c4>0. Choose epsilon0 also so
2A sqrt(epsilon0)<1/2; the inset k remains positive and
the frame sizes stay uniformly comparable to L.
Integer floor/ceiling errors are O(1), absorbed once
sqrt(epsilon)L exceeds the fixed rounding constants.

## 4. ST1: extraction and all hypotheses of the padding

restriction to P_L. The given whole-neighborhood approximation,

and inherits the affine approximation. The chosen representatives
make extraction a map even when a restriction has several extensions.

Use m=ceil(L+2A sqrt(epsilon)L) and the single rounded outer frame
described above. Its main lens contains every possible Omega_T
with the stated buffer. Here outer error E0 is bounded by C'_J,
and the source inner boundary has E1<=C1 epsilon L.
With sigma(J)>0 fixed, A3 requires

    sigma(J)c4 A sqrt(epsilon)L
       > sqrt(2)(C'_J+C1 epsilon L).

First choose epsilon0 so the epsilon L term is at most half
the left side; then choose L0(epsilon) so the constant term

Now Omega_T is the outer Jordan boundary, with error at most
C1 epsilon L, and the fixed canonical main lens is inner,
with bounded error C'_J. The A3 inequality is unchanged
because it contains the sum of those errors. Both boundaries
retain comparable words and their assigned actual integer lifts.
The translated rank box and ideals are again proved by A3;
geometric containment alone is not used to assign ranks.

Construct the A2 annulus from those boundaries once, before
varying the inner filling. Keep the reference exterior of
Omega_T unchanged. Each canonical main-lens filling now
gives an actual full tiling with precisely that exterior
and boundary lift. Restricting to the designated fixed
canonical core recovers its filling. The number of distinct
replacements is therefore at least C_k/B_J.
Choices in discarded weak end lobes are precisely why the
bounded factor is present; they are not incorrectly counted
as distinct recovered main interiors.

I checked explicitly that this argument needs only flat
canonical boundary data. Arbitrary fillings counted by C_k
need not have epsilon L affine error inside. Both old and
new piecewise affine lifts have a uniform tile-gradient bound,
and agree on the whole boundary of Omega_T.
Because Omega_T lies between positive x-monotone paths,
each vertical section is an interval. Join any interior
point vertically to one boundary path. The interval remains
inside Omega_T and has length O_J(L); integrating the
two bounded gradients proves their lift difference is O_J(L).
This supplies the claimed profile estimate without a
convexity assumption on the set of admissible profiles.

For a window of diameter O(eta p) at global scale p,
division by p gives O_J(eta). There is no extra epsilon
factor. The weaker O(eta) estimate is exactly what is
claimed. No affine-interior assertion about C_k is used
in A3 or the counting.

## 6. ST3: actual limits and the scale qualification

At fixed small epsilon,
m/L tends to 1+2A sqrt(epsilon) and
k/L tends to 1-2A sqrt(epsilon). Since the A5 entropy
limit exists at all integer frame sizes, it applies along
these floor/ceiling sequences without an additional
subsequence assumption. Dividing ST1 by L^2 gives

    limsup L^-2 log2 |F_L|
      <=(1+2A sqrt(epsilon))^2 s+K sqrt(epsilon).

The O(L) code term disappears. The fixed factor B_J
also disappears after normalization. For references
existing at every sufficiently large L, ST2 gives

    liminf L^-2 log2(replacements)
      >=(1-2A sqrt(epsilon))^2 s.

If references exist only on a subsequence, this is
only the corresponding subsequence lower assertion.
The candidate explicitly retains that distinction;
it does not manufacture a global all-scale reference.
The limit epsilon down to zero is taken after the
large-L limits with the frame, J and phase fixed.
No assertion that s is continuous under changing
slope, frame shape or rational denominators follows.

The irrational-tangent remark is valid in its stated
limited form. For a fixed strict J and nearby rational
strict J' with VJ'=I, recenter the affine offset by

    c'=c+(J-J')x0, with Vc'=0.

Then the error on the O(L) window increases by only
O(||J-J'||L). One must fix J' and its denominator,
phase and frame before L tends to infinity. This
does not supply uniform irrational-slope entropy
or a boundary-stratum theorem.
