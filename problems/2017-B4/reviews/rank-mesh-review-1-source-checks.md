## A genuine local domain issue

RMR1-01 concerns the new main text's arbitrary rectifiable-window helper,
not the external theorem. A connected window with Theta(L^2) tiny holes
has perimeter O(L) yet may touch every rank cell, even at fixed r. The
report gives an explicit D2 grid construction. A Jordan/connected-boundary
hypothesis or an explicit boundary-component term is missing. UR3's B_j
are expressly Jordan, so its own connected-curve boundary tube is valid.
The source import does not repair or require that overly broad helper.

For general D, the local alphabet has binom(D,2) types; “six” is correct
only for D4. This is an editorial qualification, separate from RMR1-01.
