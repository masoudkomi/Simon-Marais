## 4. Whole reconstruction of the actual hereditary geometry

Let t_j=2^(-j), L_j:y=t_j x-t_j², for j=1,...,n. Distinct slopes
give one transverse crossing per pair at (t_j+t_k,t_j t_k). If three
lines concur, comparison of two crossings on a fixed L_j forces
t_k=t_l, impossible. All crossings have x>0. The open regions above
all lines and below all lines are nonempty opposite unbounded cells,
so the north/south marking is legitimate.

On a vertical upward curve x=x0<0, the function t x0-t² strictly
decreases with positive t. Since t1>t2>...>tn, line heights increase
with j. This curve crosses labels1,...,n in order from south to north,
with every arrangement crossing to its right. Thus these are precisely
the canonical labels, not a relabeling chosen to make the example work.
Adding the next smaller slope gives canonical label n+1; deleting it
leaves the same marked construction. The deletion chain is hereditary
in the exact required order.

At a point not on a line let T be the set of lines above it. The strict
inequality t x-t²>y holds on an interval of the real t-axis. Hence T
is empty or a consecutive interval of j. Conversely, for a<b evaluate
the other lines at the endpoint crossing: their excess above t_a t_b
is -(t_j-t_a)(t_j-t_b). It is positive between a,b and negative outside.
Lower y by a sufficiently small epsilon; endpoints then join the
above-set and outside signs stay negative. When the interval is full,
there is no exterior restriction on epsilon. A singleton a is realized
at x=2t_a,y=t_a²-epsilon, since the other excesses are the strictly
negative -(t_j-t_a)². The empty set is obtained sufficiently high.
These arguments include every interval and all finite endpoint cases.

Each prescribed sign region is an open convex intersection of strict
affine halfplanes, so a nonempty one is one cell. For two realized
sign sets differing by one line, the segment joining interior points
keeps ALL common strict inequalities strict. It crosses only the
differing line, at a point on no other line. A small neighborhood of
that point proves an actual shared edge. Necessity of the one-line
difference follows from edge crossing. Thus Hamming-one adjacency is
proved here, not assumed for an arbitrary sign system.

North-to-south orientation adds one label. The entire directed graph
is consequently empty->each singleton, then grow a nonempty interval
at its left or right endpoint when that endpoint is available. The
full interval is south. North has outdegree n. Every other nonterminal
outdegree is1 or2. A singleton has indegree1 and an interval of length
