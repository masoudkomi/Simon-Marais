# R7 failed implications and retained arithmetic gap

2026-10-17 UTC. This is a construction record, not an independent review.

1. The suggested minimal-four-root lead is valid but partly old. T1 really
   forbids a three-VALUE cover; it is stronger than merely requiring four
   rational roots somewhere. In the actual double branch every available
   rational root is therefore essential for some derivative. Accepted RC3
   already uses this argument and obtains an exact coefficient zero under
   the old long ladder. Repeating it, or dropping the ladder only to enlarge
   the eligible index set, is not presented as a new obstruction.

2. Essential0 and essential s need not occur at the same derivative order.
   They MUST occur at different orders, because each is a sole common-root
   value there. Thus the exact higher gaps in the two translated coefficient
   arrays cannot be aligned without an additional theorem. The common
   H1 roots0/s give no such alignment. Neither H2/H4 at r nor H3/H5 at1
   supplies it.

3. The two double-root normalizations do not yield two incompatible unit
   patterns. The rational affine change fixing1 has exact coefficient
   transport a'_j=(1-s)^(-j)D_j(s). Since s has positive valuation, every
   coefficient has the same residue in both frames. More strongly, the
   weighted triple depths nu/2nu/3nu are preserved. The new R7D proves
   this obstruction to that simple reflection strategy. It does not
   construct a full polynomial satisfying the remaining constraints.

4. R6's use of a unit quadratic was stronger than its proof needed. A
   finite coefficient valuation tau<nu still makes all cubic terms vanish
   after dividing by u*r². Replacing the unit premise by this STRICT
   inequality is the new R7A comparison. One may not simply drop the
   strict inequality: when tau=nu, cubic terms can survive. The explicit
   cubic Q=4z²+3z³ satisfies the relevant two root/derivative equations
   and leading even-Taylor relation. This is only a reduced-jet diagnostic,
   not a refutation of a stronger possible global theorem.

5. The deep triple is not a contradiction. Coefficients with valuations
   at least nu,2nu,3nu are allowed in Z_(19), and may be exactly zero.
   It is false to convert a positive lower bound into an available unit.
   The alternative essential center is also not excluded; full witnesses
   can reuse1 at higher orders. The old bound1<=nu<=10 is a bound on
   an actual selected minimum in this equal-valuation setting, not a
   maximum on every displacement or coefficient depth.

6. Dividing off the rational degree-six factor does not make that factor,
   or the Eisenstein carrier, satisfy their OWN Casas-Alvero derivative
   conditions. G1 excludes every carrier root from derivative gcds; it
   does not transfer the property to a residual polynomial. The old
   degree19 critical-value polynomial is likewise not known CA. No
   smaller-degree or prime-degree theorem is applied under that gap.

7. All-recycled-roots-rational is not all-roots-rational. The fourteen
   fractional-valuation roots violate the all-root/unramified premise of
   CLO Proposition19. Real-root Birkhoff/Rolle exclusions also cannot be
   applied to unused roots without their hypotheses. The original2011
   prime5 erratum remains in force; no two-prime argument has been proved
   in this checkpoint. No universal source-proof claim is imported.
