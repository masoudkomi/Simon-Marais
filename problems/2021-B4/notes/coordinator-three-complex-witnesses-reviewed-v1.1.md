**Proved, independently reviewed (partial result)**, 2026-10-16 UTC.

If a degree-20 complex polynomial shares a root with each derivative
and at most three distinct root values can supply all those witnesses,
then it is a power of one linear polynomial. Its other roots were never
assumed to lie in that three-value set. Thus any nontrivial degree-20
example needs at least four complex recycled values.

The proof first replaces a hypothetical complex three-cover example by
an algebraic one using exact triangular integer families and an explicit
specialization for the identically-zero pair case. In a finite, possibly
ramified extension of Q_19, integral normalized coefficients and a unique
unit center yield the complete 6,680 prime-field masks. Fractional
valuations are allowed; no rational-only Newton exclusion is used.

All 6,224,952 refinements are incompatible. The old 1,493,024 exact
integer-pair algebraic incompatibilities are a separately accepted
finite dependency. Both new independent auditors reconstructed the
entire extension and independently regenerated and checked all
4,731,928 complementary pairs and every certificate. All 79 new chunks,
complete streams, coverage and separate modulo-131 misses matched.
Both initial extension reports were blind to the other conclusions.
No substantive objection or repair remained.

This reproduces the restricted claim already stated by Massri v6,
Theorem 7.10, using a different complete certificate. No novelty or
validation of Massri's separate 3^17 computation is asserted. The full
degree-20 conjecture and the annual rational-witness degree classification
remain unresolved. Four or more recycled values are not excluded here.
