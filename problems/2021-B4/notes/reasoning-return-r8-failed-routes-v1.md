# R8 genuine failed routes and precise remaining gap

2026-10-17 UTC. Candidate proof - unverified. Construction notes,
not an independent review. No annual answer or novelty claim.

1. Merely reusing minimal-four-root coverage would repeat accepted RC3
   and R7, not give a new obstruction. Essential0 and s can still occur
   at different orders. Their exact zero coefficients cannot be aligned
   without a new theorem. The new result instead analyzes three ACTUAL
   consecutive noncenter witnesses at the first break.
2. R7's quadratic proof cannot simply discard tau<nu while keeping
   all higher coefficients arbitrary. A cubic survives at the boundary.
   The new R8A condition controls that cubic coefficient explicitly and
   also requires tau<2nu, which suppresses quartic/higher terms. The
   proof supplies both strict bounds; it does not assume integrality
   after dividing by a nonunit.
3. Trying to exclude ALL cubic configurations is false at the level
   needed by the proof. The complete symbolic cubic calculation leaves
   z²(z-6) and z(z-3)², plus their two reflections, for m4 and6. They
   satisfy the root/critical/inflection and Euler residue equations.
   They are genuine obstructions to that blanket local inference, not
   actual rational or Q19 polynomial counterexamples.
4. R7's m3 diagnostic4z²+3z³ satisfies only the first TWO witness
   equations; its H2 has root8, outside0,1,2. Thus it does not obstruct
   the stronger three-order calculation. Excluding that diagnostic does
   not justify excluding the surviving m4/m6 forms or arbitrary higher
   initial forms. No finite tests or unseen local lifting theorem enter.
5. The remaining four-depth block is allowed by integrality and can
   contain zeros. It is a LOWER bound, not an upper bound on collision
   or coefficient depth. The accepted nu<=10 minimum does not change
   that. The proof is valid at every integer nu>=1 and does not create
   a finite Hensel table by misusing10 as a maximum displacement.
6. Changing double-root origins is not a contradiction. The new block
   is invariant, and the exact cubic transform pairs the exceptional
   forms. The exact gaps occur at different indices/translated values
   in the two frames, just as warned by old R7D. f itself is not assumed
   invariant under this affine map.
7. A further real/Rolle or Abel--Goncharov argument would need its actual
   all-real-root or node-order hypotheses. Four rational recycled values
   leave fourteen unused roots possibly complex; no real-root theorem
   is imported. General complex interpolation/scenario parameter counts
   remain overdetermined heuristics, not proofs of incompatibility.
8. The residual rational degree-six factor does not inherit f's CA
   property as its OWN derivatives. Neither does the irreducible degree14
   carrier; it is unrecycled. Rational witness values are not all roots,
   so CLO Proposition19 and its unramified-field extension do not apply.
9. A two-prime shortcut remains blocked by the old degree4/prime5
   phenomenon behind the2011 correction. The split residue
   x^4-x² over F5 shares0 with H1/H3 and plus/minus1 with H2;
   it cannot be dismissed by requiring rational residues. CRT alone
   does not make the19-adic and5-adic necessary conditions inconsistent.
   No new multi-prime classification or good-prime claim is proposed.

The new useful targets are specific: rule out the invariant(T4) block,
contradict one of the four rigid ACTUAL witness configurations using
additional global equations, or exclude the necessary nonunit essential
center. None follows from the present local proof. Larger routine mask,
valuation or Hensel searches were not attempted. The source literature
still leaves multi-recycled-root compatibility open in the needed scope.
