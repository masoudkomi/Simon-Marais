# Independently reviewed source objections and source-identity correction

2026-10-16 UTC. **Independently reviewed source audit; universal theorem unresolved.**
This register accepts only the narrow objections in the unchanged
[full-context note](ghosh-full-context-audit-v1.md) and
[coefficient query](ghosh-coefficient-descent-query.md), with the metadata
correction below taking precedence over their historical date attribution.
It does not accept or refute the Casas-Alvero conjecture or Theorem A.

Two fresh independent reviewers read and reconstructed the complete
22-page universal-proof PDF, inspected the disputed passages in current
HTML, checked the predecessor's distinct finiteness statements and
looked for specialized repairs throughout the paper. Both independently
verified the exact generic models. Their initial reports were frozen
blind to each other's conclusions; neither constructed the candidate.

The reviewed findings are:

- A polynomial unit g+fX over an Artinian local quotient can have nonzero
  nilpotent f. In the coefficient descent following (4.23), membership
  of one coefficient in the prime does not justify discarding its term
  modulo the smaller minors ideal. The paper supplies no checked special
  argument establishing the vanishing needed for (4.24).
- A nonzero localization does not establish that the inverted element
  was a non-zero-divisor. A torsion-freeness or saturation statement is
  needed for the specific translated derivative generators.
- A fiber of the total projective incidence closure need not equal the
  closure of its affine fiber. Finite affine fibers alone do not exclude
  escape to infinity in the neighborhood used for the degree argument.

The generic models establish these logical distinctions and preserve
specified local features; they are not actual characteristic-zero
derivative-family counterexamples. R2 additionally found a specialized
first-column identity that handles that column in one local step, but
found no extension to every required column. Its characteristic-11
experiment failed to contradict the global/local generator count and
is retained explicitly as a failed attempt. The final homology mechanism
is coherent conditional on the missing injectivity inputs. No criticism
of those conditional steps or of the separate finiteness theorem is inferred.

The strongest accepted wording is: **specific missing proof steps
identified; specialized repairs and validity of the universal theorem
remain unresolved by these audits**. This source is therefore not a
validated basis for closing the contest's open rational-witness problem.
