2026-10-18 UTC. **Candidate proof - unverified.** These routes were
worked through by hand. None is a computation or an actual CA
counterexample. The successful proposed proof is in the main.

1. **Local quadratic ramification alone.** The earlier R19 analysis
   leaves a dyadic outlier in frameII and a ramified quadratic in a
   scaled carrier. Ramification at2 can coexist with a degree14
   Eisenstein carrier at19. No global unramified-field or Galois-group
   contradiction was proved. The corrected R19 record also
   distinguishes normalized Eisenstein coordinates from the original
   rational coordinates. R20 avoids those factor conclusions entirely.

2. **The missing equation was the divided first derivative.** Looking

   local ramification. Reducing before dividing would be an invalid
   proof; Section3 proves the required order of operations.

   exactly, but D2(r)=0 is unavailable. The formal binary sequence

   by0/1. The degree19 row at the small double-root residue0 is then
   automatic. This does not construct a rational polynomial satisfying

   from being declared a frameIII exclusion.

   supplies no contradiction in the official rational-witness domain.
   Centered-moment/Hermite refinements did not give a uniform exclusion

  19-adic residue are allowed; r/19 modulo19 does not specify the

   Newer support/descent papers found in primary search were not used
