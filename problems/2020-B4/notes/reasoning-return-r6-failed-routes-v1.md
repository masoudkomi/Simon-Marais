# Failed routes and exact remaining gap: 2020 R6

2026-10-17 UTC. **Candidate proof - unverified.** Actual mathematical
failures and their repairs are retained. No routine scan or CAS was used.

1. **Universal saturation fails even for known examples.** The classical
   order-five Singer plane has v=31 and, by the accepted CLASSICAL
   normalizer result, full numerical multiplier group <5> of order
   three. Thus S0, |T|>=q, cannot be discharged for every actual plane.
   This uses attributed known existence; it is not a new construction
   of the preliminary case, and it does not assume all cyclic planes
   classical. A method conditional only on S0 cannot cover all tautness.

2. **Averaging required an unjustified coprime split.** The first scratch
   idea projected onto G^<t> by dividing a prime orbit length ell.
   That works only when ell is invertible modulo v and would have
   omitted ell|v. The final proof replaces averaging by the exact
   coinvariant A=sum(u-1)G=hG and keeps c=|A intersect H|. It works
   without gcd(|U|,v)=1. No assertion that an orbit sum is a valid
   projection is retained in the candidate.

3. **Intersection and quotient are different.** Quotient occupancies
   alone are not a smaller planar difference set. Only the actual
   intersection D intersect G^U has F1's property. The key residual
   reduction therefore needs a NONTRIVIAL pointwise residual kernel K.
   Without it, asserting that R=D minus dT has order r-1 would be false
   reasoning; no recursive multiplier theorem is then available on R.

4. **Nonfixed does not mean free.** For a general multiplier group U,
   every nonfixed orbit has size at least its smallest prime divisor,
   not necessarily |U|. A3 uses exactly that minimum. Full freeness is
   justified separately for the Baer case by every individual fixed
   group being the same H, and for the unit orbit by cancellation of
   a unit. A common fixed group alone does not prove either assertion.

5. **The old common/individual error is not reused.** Prime-order
   statements in A6 apply U=<t> itself. In D the common group equals
   the own fixed group of q only after both containments are proved.
   The old actual q4 example with T=<2> and different fixed groups for
   4 and8 remains frozen in R3/R4; its quantifier issue is not hidden.

6. **A coarse kernel bound lost a sharp small exception.** A scratch
   bound used |Aut(Z/21)|=12 and led only to a loose cutoff96. The
   actual r5 residual is much more restricted. Cohen's literal two
   generator-free exceptions each have stabilizer6; the unit case has
   orbit bound5. The final a<=6 gives the exact necessary s<=12.
   This improvement is a hand proof, not a scan of residual subsets.

7. **Faithful abelian action does not force |T|<=r.** For example an
   abstract C3 x C3 acts faithfully on two disjoint three-point orbits:
   the two coordinate quotient actions have trivial common kernel.
   Its order9 exceeds the degree6. This is an abstract permutation
   counterexample only, not an actual difference set. The valid bound
   is the product of residual orbit lengths, at most3^(r/3).

8. **The strict threshold matters.** At the known actual order16,
   <2> has size12 and the residual five-point set of a unit orbit is
   fixed by64. Thus its residual action is not faithful. The candidate
   preserves this example and states s>12, not s>=12. It does not say
   that the logarithmic inequality itself fails at12.

9. **An earlier parameter-table inference was rejected.** Scratch
   discussion of the old q2435 necessary-test record tried to infer a
   modulus property from failure of an orbit divisibility condition.
   Gordon's published Table1 actually labels its prime factor explicitly.
   A NONEXISTENT parameter record cannot be treated as an ACTUAL plane,
   let alone as a counterexample. No primality, existence or new exclusion
   claim about that record is used here; no old table was recomputed.

10. **Necessary matrices do not supply actual incidence.** The coordinator's
    separate quadratic-character matrix lead was read as an explicitly
    UNVERIFIED context note. It supplies possible abstract Gram data,
    not the prime-field cyclotomic matrix or an actual planar set. It is
    not a premise of R6A--R6D. No abstract realization is promoted.

11. **The flag-transitivity classification has extra hypotheses.** Accepted
    R5's even saturated branch leads to the specific non-Desarguesian
    flag-transitive obstruction. Xia's reverse reduction is not a theorem
    about all cyclic planes or all multiplier subgroups. The odd affine
    group has two flag orbits, without restricting the full automorphism
    group enough to conclude nonexistence. No CFSG shortcut is used.

The exact remaining nonsaturated gap is arithmetic incidence across the
residual orbits. At prime v all nonidentity fixed subgroups are {0}, so
the fixed-subplane descent alone gives no smaller nontrivial plane.
The faithful-action logarithmic lower bound still permits large residual
sets; character magnitudes and orbit cardinalities leave their phases
and individual differences uncontrolled. There is no argument here that
these admissible residuals cannot occur at non-prime-power q. A genuinely
stronger next step would have to constrain the ACTUAL inter-orbit
difference maps, rather than replace them by their augmentations or
positive-definite norms.
