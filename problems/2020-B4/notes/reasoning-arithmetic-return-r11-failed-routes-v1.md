# R11 discarded strengthenings and exact remaining gap

2026-10-18 UTC. **Candidate reasoning - unverified.** These are actual
routes considered while deriving A11A--E. No parameter, class-number,
norm-form or mask scan was used.

## 1. Minimal zero-sum alone excludes mixed cancellation

It does not. Accepted M10 gives actual mixed principal products at
large supported norms and the hand-complete Qsqrt(-31) example. The
new argument has to exploit the EXACT critical p=Phi3(q), which makes
every proper supported nonsquare norm impossible after unit correction.
It does not refute M10 or replace its actual ideal realizations by
abstract class labels. A11C is stronger than minimality because it
also tests relations containing negative signs.

## 2. Equal class sums give the needed smaller norm by division

The naive quotient B_S/B_T is fractional and has rational norm ratio
q_S/q_T. Even when its class is zero, neither integrality nor a positive
integer norm follows. This would break the small-norm application.
The valid construction cancels common indices in CLASSES, then uses
B_(S\T)*c(B_(T\S)). It is integral and has the UNION norm, with no
denominator. This was the decisive repair in the reasoning before
the candidate was written; no earlier frozen theorem asserted the
invalid quotient step.

## 3. Every collision must already contradict a proper norm

The symmetric difference can be ALL prime indices. Its norm is then
q, at the permitted equality boundary of the source estimate. This
is why complementary collisions survive. They are not an annoyance
that can be discarded: an actual norm-q product gives exactly such
an empty/full collision, and a forced complementary collision is the
new positive existence mechanism.

## 4. There may be many complementary collision pairs

Without checking group parity, one might allow one pair above every
class. In the stated odd group every common value must solve2y=w,
so there is only one possible value, and its fibre has at most2
members. This gives at most ONE pair in the entire power set.
If2-torsion were present that unique-half argument would fail, and
the preceding norm-unit bridge also need not be available. No even
ordinary class-number extension is claimed.

## 5. Treat every repeated-prime layer as an odd block

Two layers of the same rational prime can have square union norm.
The published proper-norm obstruction does not apply to squares.
The main therefore groups the ENTIRE ell_i^e_i factor into one
balanced block, and its all-odd theorem requires every e_i odd.
It does not pretend B_i is necessarily a power of one norm-ell_i
balanced ideal. In the mixed case it counts only the odd-exponent
blocks, retaining an omitted even block to keep their union proper.

The simplest square obstruction to an overstatement is a rational
integer: its principal ideal is balanced of square norm. No amount
of class labeling can turn that into a forbidden nonsquare norm.
Conversely, a SPECIFIED actual even block need not be that rational
ideal. Replacing it by a principal rational block changes the total
class, and dividing an actual character by the rational square root
of the even part need not preserve integrality. Thus the odd-block
restriction does not reduce the full existence problem to the odd part.

## 6. Use the generated subgroup as if it were the whole minus group

H depends on actual balanced choices and may be a proper subgroup
of G. The necessary bound and the pigeonhole implication apply to
H itself. The relative-class-number criterion is a stronger sufficient
hypothesis because H<=G; it is not an asserted equality in general.
Even when an actual solution has2^t-1 distinct subset sums, those
values need not fill a larger H. No inverse additive theorem or
classification of such near-complete subset-sum systems is proved.

## 7. A small-class-group sufficient theorem verifies a new order

No new cyclotomic field is shown to satisfy the size condition at
a non-prime-power q. It may be excluded in many cases by independent
arithmetic. The theorem is an actual conditional solvability bridge,
not an example or a promise that its hypotheses occur. Conversely,
large G or failure of the size condition does not exclude tautness.
There may still be one successful balanced ideal among many choices.

## 8. Replace the actual multiplier field by a convenient quadratic one

A larger odd subgroup can give a smaller field and a stronger norm
form, but its containment of the prime-divisor residues does not mean
it stabilizes a pre-existing D. The actual known subgroup and the full
numerical group must remain distinguished. An attractive quadratic
norm estimate cannot be imposed on a character that is not in that
quadratic field. No cyclic=>classical or full-automorphism assertion
is introduced to repair this missing inclusion.

## 9. Use field norms, positivity or ideal balance as the missing generator

These are three different levels. A field norm may have denominators.
A balanced ideal may be nonprincipal. A principal balanced ideal gives
a positive unit factor that must be a UNIT norm. The accepted odd
ordinary class argument supplies the last correction and preserves
the same ideal, but supplies no universal principality. A11A first
constructs an integral ideal, proves its class zero, and only then
uses the correction. The old q5 nonprincipal ideal remains nonprincipal.

## 10. Obtain critical size from the old Chebotarev or weighted lift

M10 gives actual large supported N in a prescribed residue class,
not the fixed product of prime divisors of q. Its exact weighted
defect at N=q+hp is positive for h>=1. A11E avoids that unsupported
step by using ONLY the specified primary blocks of q. The final
identity k²-q=p makes the group-ring multiplier exactly1. No
primitive character at composite Phi3(N) is made into a full jointly
integral tuple, and no bound for arbitrary weighted lifts becomes
a new positive-configuration exclusion.

## 11. Treat searches or old source records as a new complete theorem audit

Primary searches for cyclotomic class groups, zero-sum patterns and
planar difference-set restrictions did not supply a new theorem closing
the residual class-size problem. That is not a literature-absence or
priority claim. The exact operative small-norm theorem/proof, cyclic
Hasse statement/proof and local-unit propositions were actually read.
The original ambiguous-class preprint was read, but no entire CFT
foundation or newly discovered general classification is claimed.
Schmidt1998's unread current primary proof is not imported; the old
accepted M10 source diagnostic remains limited to its written scope.

## 12. What still must be controlled

The actual class groups at the specified split primes can be large.
The new signed restriction does not prove an upper bound meeting
its own pigeonhole threshold. Even ordinary class-number fields and
composite polygon moduli retain their old extra obstructions. A proof
excluding every surviving mixed principal product would need more
arithmetic information; a new unconditional positive family would
need verified hypotheses at actual q. Neither is supplied here. The
candidate requires TWO OTHER whole audits before any promotion.
