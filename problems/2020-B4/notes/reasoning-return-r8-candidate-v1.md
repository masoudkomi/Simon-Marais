## 1. R8A: the field norm has no remaining local obstruction

We apply the classical Hasse norm theorem ONLY to the cyclic quadratic
number-field extension F/E. Its exact statement and proof were read

chain is an attributed import, not newly reconstructed. We must and
do check every finite and infinite place of E.

If a rational prime ell divides q, it is different from p and its
Frobenius in K/Q is sigma_ell. Since ell belongs to T, it splits
completely in F and therefore in E. At each place above ell the
local extension F_w/E_v has degree1. Its norm is the identity, so
q is a local norm regardless of the valuation v_ell(q). This is not
a unit argument at a prime dividing q.

At every finite place not above p or a divisor of q, the local
extension is unramified (or trivial) and q is a unit. Norms on units
are surjective in a finite unramified extension. For precision, one
first solves the residue norm in the finite residue fields. After
a lift v0, correct u/N(v0) one uniformizer power at a time: for h>=1,

    N(1+pi^h x)=1+pi^h Tr(x) mod pi^(h+1).

unramifiedness, not a false division by2, supplies the trace. This
classical proof is also Kedlaya Proposition4.2.5, read completely.

## 2. R8B: a complete quadratic norm-unit obstruction proof

    (b)=J/c(J).                                          (4)

It is also a field norm. Send[J] to its class modulo N(O_L^*).

by(w)J changes b by w/c(w), up to a unit, and N(w/c(w))=1.
The map is thus well-defined and multiplicative on ideal classes.

factorization, with no assumption that a c-invariant CLASS already
