# B4 setters and mathematical origins

Checked 2026-10-16 UTC. “Proposed by” identifies the competition proposer;
it does not establish authorship of the underlying research conjecture.
Committee membership, chairmanship, and similarity to someone's research
are not setter evidence. Unknown entries below remain unknown.

## Committee chronology

The current [official About Us page](https://www.simonmarais.org/aboutus.html)
explicitly labels its problem committee as **2026**. It lists Sean Gardiner
as chair and Anand Deopurkar, Norman Do, Ivan Guo, James Katende, Ka Ho
Law, Ramiro Lafuente, Dana Ma, Peter McNamara and Christopher Tuffley.
This roster must not be backdated to 2017–2025. Historical organizer
reports support specific earlier chair appointments, not a complete
year-by-year membership list or setter attribution.

The [2017 first-person McNamara blog post](https://petermc.net/blog/2017/10/19/simon-marais-problem-competition-2017/)
confirms his membership and credits a different problem to himself.
It supplies no B4 credit. No mathematics of that different problem is
investigated here.

The [University of the Free State research profile](https://www.ufs.ac.za/natagri/departments-and-divisions/mathematics-and-applied-mathematics-home/research/research-profiles)
identifies Meyer's near-ring and combinatorial research. That profile
does not connect a published theorem to this recurrence. The organizer's
explicit origin account is stronger evidence than a thematic inference.

Further searches continue. No messages have been sent to setters or organizers.
